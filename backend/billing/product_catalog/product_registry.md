# Product Registry

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Canonical
**Architecture Phase:** 2.1.1
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-25

---

# Purpose

The Product Registry is the canonical registry for every billable product offered by LinkCraftor.

No product may be sold, subscribed to, or billed unless it is registered here.

---

# Registry Objectives

- Single Product Registry
- Canonical Product Definitions
- Version Controlled Products
- Product Lifecycle Management
- Product Traceability
- Billing Integration
- API Integration
- Marketplace Integration
- OEM Integration

---

# Registered Product Categories

- Subscription Plans
- API Products
- Marketplace Products
- OEM Products
- Professional Services
- Training Products
- Add-On Products
- Future Products

---

# Product Requirements

Every registered product shall have:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Product Category
- Product Version
- Product Status
- Billing Model
- Pricing Model
- Feature Mapping
- Availability
- Effective Date
- Retirement Date (if applicable)

- Active
