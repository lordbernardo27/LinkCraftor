# Purpose

This document defines the governance framework for the Universal Billing Architecture.

It establishes ownership, accountability, approval processes, operational controls, compliance requirements and change governance for every billing component within LinkCraftor.

---

# Governance Objectives

- Ensure consistent billing decisions
- Protect billing integrity
- Maintain financial accuracy
- Support regulatory compliance
- Control billing changes
- Maintain auditability
- Preserve architectural consistency
- Protect customer trust

---

# Governance Principles

- Centralized Ownership
- Separation of Duties
- Least Privilege
- Change Approval
- Version Control
- Auditability
- Traceability
- Financial Integrity
- Compliance by Design
- Continuous Governance

---

# Governance Scope

The governance framework applies to:

- Pricing
- Subscription Plans
- Discounts
- Promotions
- Action Units (AU)
- Document Top-Ups
- API Billing
- Payment Providers
- Invoices
- Credits
- Wallets
- Revenue Reporting
- Enterprise Billing
- OEM Licensing
- Marketplace Billing

---

# Governance Requirements

Every billing change shall:

- Be formally reviewed
- Be approved before release
- Be version controlled
- Be documented
- Be auditable
- Be reversible where applicable
- Be validated before production deployment
- Preserve financial integrity
