**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Canonical
**Architecture Phase:** 1.1.3
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-25

---

# Purpose

This document defines the mandatory standards governing the implementation, operation, security, and maintenance of the Universal Billing Architecture.

All billing components shall comply with these standards.

---

# Billing Standards

## 1. Canonical Billing Standard

All billing shall be processed exclusively through the Universal Billing Architecture.

---

## 2. Pricing Standard

Pricing shall be centrally managed and version controlled.

---

## 3. Subscription Standard

All subscription lifecycle events shall follow the canonical subscription engine.

---

## 4. Usage Metering Standard

Every billable action shall be accurately metered before invoicing.

---

## 5. Ledger Standard

Every financial transaction shall be permanently recorded in an immutable billing ledger.

---

## 6. Audit Standard

Every billing event shall generate a complete audit trail.

---

## 7. Security Standard

Billing operations shall enforce authentication, authorization, encryption, and audit logging.

---

## 8. Configuration Standard

Billing behavior shall be configuration-driven rather than hard coded.

---

## 9. Integration Standard

All LinkCraftor products and services shall integrate with the Universal Billing Architecture through approved interfaces.

---

## 10. Scalability Standard

The billing platform shall support horizontal scaling and enterprise workloads without architectural changes.
