**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Canonical
**Architecture Phase:** 1.1.4

---

# Purpose

This document defines the mandatory billing policies governing all monetized products, services and transactions within LinkCraftor.

All billing implementations shall comply with these policies.

---

# Billing Policies

## 1. Single Billing Authority

The Universal Billing Architecture is the only approved billing authority.

---

## 2. Subscription Policy

Every paid subscription shall be managed exclusively through the Subscription Engine.

---

## 3. Pricing Policy

All prices shall be centrally managed and version controlled.

---

## 4. Usage Policy

Every billable action shall be accurately metered before billing.

---

## 5. Action Unit Policy

Application AU Top-Ups and API Action Unit billing shall follow their approved pricing models.

---

## 6. Invoice Policy

Every charge shall generate an auditable invoice or financial record.

---

## 7. Payment Policy

Only approved payment providers may process LinkCraftor transactions.

---

## 8. Refund Policy

Refunds, credits and adjustments shall be recorded through the billing ledger and never by modifying historical transactions.

---

## 9. Security Policy

All billing information shall be protected through authentication, authorization, encryption and audit logging.

---

## 10. Compliance Policy

Billing operations shall comply with applicable financial, tax and regulatory requirements.
