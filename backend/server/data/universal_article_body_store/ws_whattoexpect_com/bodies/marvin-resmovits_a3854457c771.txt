About Marvin Resmovits, M.D.

Dr. Marvin Resmovits is a board-certified pediatrician in Great Neck, New York, and serves as Chief of the Western Division of Pediatrics at Optum. Dr. Resmovits has been a practicing primary care pediatrician for more than 35 years.

Dr. Resmovits did his pediatric residency at Long Island Jewish Medical Center, in the pediatric program now referred to as Cohen Children's Medical Center, and served as Assistant Chief Resident in his third year.

He has a Bachelor of Science degree from Yeshiva University in New York City, and received his M.D. from the State University of New York at Buffalo.

Board certification

Dr. Resmovits is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Resmovits is affiliated with Cohen Children's Medical Center in New York, New York, and North Shore University Hospital in Manhasset, New York.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Resmovits is an assistant clinical professor of Pediatrics at the Hofstra Northwell School of Medicine in Hempstead, New York. He is also a member of the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP).

Awards

Dr. Resmovits has been a Castle Connolly Top Doctor for more than 20 years.
