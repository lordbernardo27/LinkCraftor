What is Hib disease?

The Hib vaccine protects your baby from Hib disease, or Haemophilus influenzae type b. This bacteria can cause a variety of infections, including ear infections, bloodstream infections, and even pneumonia. Hib disease used to be a leading cause of bacterial meningitis, an infection of the tissue covering the spinal cord and brain, in children under 5. What is the Hib vaccine?

There are four Hib vaccines licensed for use in the U.S. Three of the vaccines protect against Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib) disease only, while one vaccine is a combination vaccine, which means it includes protection against other diseases as well.
