Finding the perfect cute and unique baby boy name for your future son is a huge responsibility, but you can do it! Find some inspiration with these top boy names.

Choosing a baby boy name is one of the first important decisions you'll make as a parent-to-be. Should you select a traditional name that will stand the test of time, possibly one that's remained in your family for generations? Or, should you opt for a more modern and unique baby boy name ?

Fortunately, there are plenty of resources to help you choose the perfect name for your son. And believe it or not, the Social Security Administration (SSA) is a great place to start. Every year, the SSA rounds up the 1,000 most popular baby girl and boy names. The list below references SSA data from 2025.

Can't decide on the perfect name for your new bundle of joy? Run your ideas by other moms on the What to Expect app.

Here, browse the list of the top baby boy names for inspiration (or to see if your little one's name made the list!).

Key Takeaways

Choosing a baby name is a big deal, and whether you're going traditional or trendy, there's a massive list to explore thanks to the Social Security Administration's annual roundup.

The top boy names in 2025 include favorites like Liam, Noah, Oliver, and Theodore, but more unique names like Kasai, Akari, and Eziah are also rising.

With so many options to explore, this list is a great starting point whether you're naming your baby boy, looking for inspiration, or just curious about name trends.

How are the top baby boy names chosen?

Every year, the SSA compiles their list of the top baby names for boys and girls using data from U.S. Social Security Card applications. The most recent data available is for 2025, which the list above references.
