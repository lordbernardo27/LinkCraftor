About Sipra Laddha, M.D.

Sipra Laddha, M.D., is a board-certified physician and psychiatrist in the Atlanta, Georgia, area who is trained in psychopharmacology, psychodynamic psychotherapy, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), and humanistic psychotherapy. Dr. Laddha specializes in perinatal psychiatry and women's mental health. She got her medical degree from the University at Buffalo School of Medicine.

Dr. Laddha co-founded LunaJoy Health , focused on holistic women's mental health services, where she is working to rapidly increase awareness and access around common (but rarely addressed) mental health struggles during vulnerable life transitions.

Board certifications

Dr. Laddha is board-certified by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Laddha is affiliated with Atlanta Veterans Affairs Medical Center in Decatur, Georgia.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Laddha trained at the Emory University School of Medicine residency program in Atlanta, and served as chief resident there.

She is the mom of four children, including a set of twins, who have been her biggest motivators and teachers.
