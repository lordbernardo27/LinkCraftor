About Shubhangi Kesavan, M.D.

Dr. Shubhangi Kesavan is a board-certified OB/GYN practicing in Cleveland, Ohio.

Dr. Kesavan completed her OB/GYN residency at Rutgers University in New Jersey and received her M.D. from the Pad. Dr. D.Y. Patil Medical college in India. She is currently an OB Laborist and Staff Physician at Cleveland Clinic in Ohio.

Her practice interests include high-risk obstetrics management, breastfeeding support, and contraception management.

Board certifications

Dr. Kesavan is board-certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology and is a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG).

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Kesavan is affiliated with Fairview Hospital and Hillcrest Hospital, part of Cleveland Clinic's main campus in Cleveland.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Kesavan is a Clinical Assistant Professor and OBAI Director at Cleveland Clinic Lerner College of Medicine, affiliated with Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland. She serves on several committees for the Society of OB/GYN Hospitalists.

Dr. Kesavan lives with her husband and daughter.

Publications and research

Dr. Kesavan has authored many book chapters, including Merck Manuals and Elsevier publications.

Awards

Dr. Kesavan received a CCLM Pin Recognition for year 3 clerkship in OB/GYN for excellence in teaching in 2023.
