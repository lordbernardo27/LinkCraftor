About Gina Posner, M.D., F.A.A.P.

Dr. Gina Posner is a board-certified pediatrician practicing at MemorialCare Medical Group in Fountain Valley, California.

Dr. Posner completed her residency at White Memorial Medical Center in Los Angeles — where she was chief resident — and she received her M.D. from New York Medical College in Valhalla, New York. She previously graduated from Brandeis University in Waltham, Massachusetts, with a degree in International Health and the Environment.

Board certification

Dr. Posner has been board-certified with the American Board of Pediatrics since 2006, and is a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Posner is affiliated with Orange Coast Memorial Medical Center in Fountain Valley, California.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Posner serves as the Assistant Chair of Pediatrics at Orange Coast Memorial Medical Center. She is bilingual in Spanish and English.

Dr. Posner is the mother of two daughters.

Awards

Dr. Posner received the National Health Service Corps Scholarship, providing health services in an underserved area after completing her residency. She provided medical care at Clinica Sierra Vista in Lamont, California, during her time in the National Health Service Corps program.
