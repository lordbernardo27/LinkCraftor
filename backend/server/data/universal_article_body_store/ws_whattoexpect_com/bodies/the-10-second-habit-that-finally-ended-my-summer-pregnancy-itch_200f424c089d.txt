I pictured a glowy third-trimester summer: breezy linen dresses and endless frozen treats. The reality was very different. Within seconds of stepping outside, I was drenched in sweat, my swelling was out of control, and the constant skin-on-skin friction made my thighs feel like they were covered in angry mosquito bites. I didn’t want to leave the house.

I tried every hack the TikTok algorithm threw my way. Cooling towels left me damp, handheld fans did nothing for the irritation, and body powders turned cakey within the hour. Nothing actually solved the problem, which was friction. Eventually, I gave in and tried the Megababe Thigh Rescue Anti-Chafe Stick , a $15 balm that had been popping up in my feed for months with influencers calling it a summer holy grail. I held off at first because I assumed it was just a glorified deodorant (and I had already tried the deodorant trick without success), but by then I was willing to try anything.

Within days, I was buying backups for my car and nightstand. It stopped the itch before it started, and despite the name, I used it everywhere: under my breasts, along the underside of my bump, anywhere sweat and fabric were constantly rubbing. Unlike standard deodorant, it’s actually built for friction. It creates a smooth, residue-free barrier that keeps skin from chafing, and the light citrus scent was refreshing without being overwhelming, even with pregnancy nose.

The biggest win was how easy it was to use. By the third trimester, even basic movements feel like a workout, and the glide-on formula meant I didn’t have to contort myself to reach the spots that needed it. It didn’t fix the heat or the swelling, but it got rid of the part that made me the most uncomfortable.

If you’re pregnant in the summer and dealing with thigh, under-boob, or under-bump irritation, this is one of those things I’d actually tell a close friend to buy. It takes two seconds to use and makes a very real difference when you’re hot, uncomfortable, and just trying to get out of the house.
