About Tina Feeley, M.D., M.P.H.

Dr. Tina Feeley is a board-certified pediatrician practicing at Chestnut Hill Pediatrics in Chestnut Hill, Massachusetts.

After graduating from Boston College with a Bachelor of Science in Biology, Dr. Feeley went on to earn her Masters in Public Health from Dartmouth Medical School. She then earned an M.D. with honors from Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School in New Brunswick, New Jersey.

Dr. Feeley completed her pediatric residency at the Children's Hospital of The King's Daughters in Norfolk, Virginia. She later worked as a general pediatrician at Naval Medical Center Portsmouth, Virginia — taking care of the children of active duty service members — before moving to Boston and starting her current position at Chestnut Hill Pediatrics.

Board certifications

Dr. Feeley is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and a member of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Feeley is affiliated with Boston Children’s Hospital in Boston, Massachusetts.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Feeley previously served as an assistant professor of pediatrics, teaching Navy and Air Force pediatric residents and medical students from the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences in Bethesda, Maryland.

Awards

Dr. Feeley is a member of both the Alpha Omega Alpha Honor Society and the Gold Humanism Honor Society.
