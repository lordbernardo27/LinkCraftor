How What to Expect Chooses the Best Products for Parents

Shopping for baby gear can be exciting — but it’s also one of the most overwhelming parts of preparing for parenthood. In fact, 1 in 3 moms say there are “too many baby product options” out there.¹ You’ve got enough on your plate (and in your cart). That’s where What to Expect comes in — not just as a trusted resource, but as your smart-shopping sidekick.

Whether you’re stumped by which pregnancy pillow to buy or on the hunt for a breast pump that actually fits your lifestyle, we bring expert insight, real-parent reviews, and medically reviewed advice — all tailored to what matters most to you.

Why Parents Trust What to Expect

What to Expect is the #1 most trusted source for product guidance among moms 18 to 44 — ranking ahead of social media, influencers, friends, and family.¹ That trust shapes everything we do.

Our product recommendations are rooted in real-life parent insight, informed by medical professionals, and shaped by the feedback of millions of parents and parents-to-be in our community. Editors follow rigorous research standards, and every guide is reviewed by vetted OB/GYNs, pediatricians, or other credible experts — ensuring that every pick is safe, reliable, and truly helpful for your growing family.

Behind the Scenes: How We Vet New Products

Each product undergoes multiple checks — from scanning for safety recalls, to expert board review, to real-world staff testing. Our internal litmus test? “Would I use this for my own baby?”

If the answer’s yes, only then does the product make it into our guides.

How We Cover Sales

Raising a child comes with costs — and smart savings matter. That’s why we only feature price drops that offer real value and reflect the lowest pricing we’ve seen in the past three months.

We don’t report on every single sale. Instead, we focus on deals that help parents save on gear they actually need .
