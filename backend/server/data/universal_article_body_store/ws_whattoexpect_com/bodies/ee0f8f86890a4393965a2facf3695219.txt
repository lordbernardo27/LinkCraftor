Causes of Crooked or Uneven Nail Extensions

Before jumping into the solution, let’s find the root cause first. (1) Multiple factors can change the appearance of your nail extensions. This can include improper placement of the nail forms. The misplacement can happen when you apply the nail tip from a wrong angle. Other causes can include the following:

Too much product application on one side and very little on the other

Bumpy surfaces

Poor nail preparation

Sometimes, improper filing can also cause uneven or crooked nail extensions. Therefore, nail experts always recommend evenly filling the sidewalls and carefully checking for alignment when shaping your nails.

How to Fix? The Quick Solution to Crooked Nail Extensions

When fixing crooked or uneven nail extensions, you have to maintain a keen eye throughout the process. Here is a step-by-step guide for your help:

Step 1. Identify the Issue

As discussed above, you have to first find the (2) root cause of your nail problem. Is it due to misalignment, improper filling, or uneven thickness of the gel layers? Once you’ve identified the issue, you can move forward with the next fixation steps.

Step 2. Use a Filing Technique

When dealing with crooked or uneven nails, you have to be very careful. (3) Using a filing technique can help solve the issue but it should be done with utmost care and safety. If done wrong, it can damage your natural nails and affect your overall appearance.

When using this technique, start by using the right tools. For instance, your nail file should be perfect to not (7) damage the natural nail layers. Here are some nail files you can look for:

180-Grit File

240-Grit or Higher

Nail Buffer

Once you have your ideal nail file, start filing the nail extensions in one direction. It will help you in taking better control and avoid over-filing in an area. For uneven extensions, you should correct the length in such a way that the longer nails match with the shorter ones.

Similarly, focus on the sidewalls and create a balanced shape. (10) For bumpy surfaces, you can also use a buffing block. This will help remove inconsistencies. Plus, avoid removing too much product as it can also affect your nail extensions.

3. Use Natural Remedies

When it comes to quick fixes, natural remedies have always helped us. These natural yet simple methods allow you to correct crooked or uneven nail extensions without using toxic chemicals or taking professional help. For instance, you can use a clear gel to naturally level extensions.

Source: https://www.salongeek.com/threads/crooked-nails-you-or-them.310440/

A fine brush can also be helpful in this process. You can coat a thin layer of gel on the nail or focus on the areas that need balance. For this, simply place your hand under natural sunlight or an LED lamp to cure the gel. For severe unevenness, keep applying thin layers until the surface gets leveled. Besides, here are some other natural remedies that you can use:

Aloe Vera Gel

Aloe Vera Gel is an all-in-one quick-fix remedy that can hydrate and smooth uneven extensions. Many people with crooked nail extensions prefer this gel over toxic chemicals to avoid harsh skin reactions. Using it is simple.

Source: https://www.freepik.com/premium-ai-image/photo-hydrating-aloe-vera-gel_140717466.htm

You have to apply a thin layer of pure aloe vera gel over the nail. (4) Usually, it is advised to apply the gel for almost 20 minutes to gently file the nail surface. Then, let it dry completely, and buff lightly for a smoother texture. Although it is perfect for minor imperfections but do not guarantee long-lasting results.

Nail Glue and Powder

Now, what to do if you have smaller cracks in your nail extensions? Nothing much, simply apply a small amount of (11) nail glue to the uneven area and spread a small quantity of nail repair powder over the glue.

You can use a natural alternative or baking soda as a nail repair powder. This will smooth the surface and correct the uneven nail extensions. Once the nail is dried, use a fine-grit buffer and balance the entire crooked surface.

Natural Oils

Among natural remedies, you can use a small amount of cuticle or natural oil for further adjustments. This can include oils like Jojoba or vitamin E as they will smoothen the cuticle area and allow the oil to absorb fully and rehydrate the surface. You can always add a non-toxic topcoat that will add a smooth and glossy finish.

Key Takeaways

Now, you have a detailed look at (6) how to fix crooked nail extensions, let’s rewind the key points one by one below:

Start by identifying the root causes that led to crooked or uneven nail extensions.

How to Avoid Future Issues Related to Crooked or Uneven Nail Extensions

Now that you have got a detailed step-by-step guide on how to fix crooked or uneven nails, it’s better to prepare for the future. As they say, prevention is better than cure. Here are some practical steps you can take to avoid these problems in the future:

Consult a Nail Technician

It’s always great to keep in touch with a nail technician. However, be very careful when selecting a nail expert. You can check recommendations and ask for reviews to ensure the specific person has extensive experience and is qualified enough to give you better consultation.

Nail Preparation

To avoid future issues, pay high attention to your nail preparation. One practical way is to clean and dry the nails. You should remove any dirt, residue, or debris from your nail extensions. Do the same with your natural nails. This will create a rough texture and prepare your nail extensions from bending or unevenness.

For quick fixes and safety, apply extensions at home or carefully observe your technician. When doing so, follow the natural curve of your finger. Smoothly apply the acrylic or nail gel for a better texture.

Use Quality Products

When doing skin care, always invest in high-quality tools and products. You can use professional-grade tools with consistent grits. When using gel products, always prefer reputable brands for a smooth and even application.

Above all, take your time and do not rush through the process. Doing so can lead to mistakes so give yourself enough time for each step. Whether you’re fixing crooked nail extensions at home or at a salon, do it with extreme care and caution.

FAQs

Can I fix uneven nail extensions at home?

Yes, you can easily fix uneven nail extensions at home using simple tools. (9) For minor unevenness, you can use a nail file or a liquid gel such as aloe vera. This way, you can smooth the uneven surfaces and fix your crooked nail extensions.

Why Are My Nail Extensions Crooked?

Crooked nail extensions can be due to multiple reasons. (8) The most common one includes misaligned application, uneven layering or improper nail preparation. Sometimes, issues with shaping or filling can also cause crooked or uneven nail extensions.

What tools do I need to fix uneven nail extensions?

There is no fixed list of tools. You only need a fine nail file for shaping, a buffing block for smoothness, a clear gel for filling uneven areas, and a LED gel for curing gel if needed. You can use additional tools but make sure all of them are high-quality products to avoid skin reactions.

Is it safe to fix nail extensions myself?

When there are minor issues like uneven length, you can fix them yourself. However, if there is any severe lifting or unevenness, you can take help from a professional to avoid further damage. Usually, it’s recommended to consult a nail expert if you’re unsure how to handle the problem.

How can I prevent nail extensions from becoming uneven in the future?

To avoid uneven extensions in the future, you can keep in touch with a nail technician. Another way is to learn the right strategy if you’re doing it at home. Above all, make sure you have carefully done the nail preparation and frequently check during the alignment.
