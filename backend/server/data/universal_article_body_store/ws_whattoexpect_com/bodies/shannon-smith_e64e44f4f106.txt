About Shannon Smith, M.D.

Dr. Shannon Smith is a board-certified OB/GYN and partner at Brigham Faulkner Ob/Gyn Associates in Boston, Massachusetts.

Dr. Smith has a B.A. degree in psychology with a distinction of honors from Wake Forest University. She earned her M.D. from Wake Forest University School of Medicine and completed her residency and training at Tufts Medical Center in Boston.

Board certifications

Dr. Smith is board-certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Smith is affiliated with Brigham and Women’s Hospital and Brigham and Women’s Faulkner Hospital in Boston.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Smith is a faculty member at Harvard Medical School and a fellow of the American Congress of Obstetricians & Gynecologists (FACOG). She's also a member of the American College of Obstetrics & Gynecology (ACOG), the American Medical Association (AMA), the Massachusetts Medical Association (MMS) and the American Association of Gynecologic Laparoscopists (AAGL).

Publications

Dr. Smith has published a variety of abstracts and online articles covering subjects related to pregnancy and minimally invasive surgery.

Awards

Dr. Smith has been honored as a Top Doctor in Massachusetts since 2015.
