What Are Some Fun Food Ideas for a Gender Reveal Party?

gender reveal food ideas, ice cream cone with sprinkles

From pink-and-blue treats to pregnancy-safe snacks and fun reveal desserts, here are some food ideas to make your gender reveal party memorable.

A gender reveal party is a great opportunity to have some fun with food. Create a menu that matches your gender reveal theme , or stick with a classic pink-and-blue palette. Think foods that are naturally pink or blue (like strawberries or blueberries) and foods you can customize (with pink or blue icing or sprinkles, for example).

This is such an exciting time for you and your loved ones, so make sure their stomachs are as full as their hearts. Read on for ideas for gender reveal drinks, desserts, and more.

Key Takeaways

Stick to a fun, themed menu — especially pink and blue options. Gender reveal foods often follow a pink-and-blue color scheme, with customizable treats like cupcakes, cookies, cake pops, and drinks that match the theme or reveal the baby’s sex.

Offer a mix of sweet treats, light bites, and drinks. A well-rounded menu includes desserts, finger foods, and themed drinks or mocktails to keep guests satisfied.

Prioritize pregnancy-safe and guest-friendly foods. Make sure the menu includes foods that are safe for the mom-to-be while still appealing to guests, keeping the event enjoyable and inclusive for everyone.

What foods should I serve at our gender reveal?

In many ways, this is your day. Plan a menu that everyone will enjoy — including pregnancy-safe foods for you and your baby. That means skipping items like cheese boards, sushi, and deli meats (save those for baby's first birthday bash).

What are some blue and pink food ideas?

These classic colors offer plenty of inspiration for your menu. Choose food that are blue or pink, or customize any party foods with colors. Here are some ideas:

Blue and pink cookies

gender reveal onesie cookies

Complete with a family name, these cookies are outfitted with a onesie design and serve as an adorable addition to any gender reveal party.

"It was fun to see some people walk around with all blue or pink plates sticking to what gender they thought it was," says What to Expect Community mom elizabethh01.

Berries

For a lighter option, serve strawberries or raspberries for pink and blueberries for blue.

Rice Krispies pops

gender reveal rice krispies pops

These chocolate-covered crisped rice treats, decorated with baby footprints, are an oh-so-sweet (and gluten-free) item to serve your guests.

Personalized M&M's

Custom-order M&M's in pink, blue, or a mix, and add a personalized message for décor and dessert in one.

Smash boxes

gender reveal smash boxes

For a dramatic reveal, smash a chocolate box filled with blue or pink candies.

Cheesecake cups

gender reveal cheesecake cups

These individually prepared blue and pink cheesecake cups come adorned with gold pacifiers, teddy bears, and tiny footprints.

Cake pops

Put a twist on the classic gender reveal cake by handing out cake pops. Bonus: You can even turn it into a game by revealing baby's sex in just one of the cake pops. Go around the room and have each guest bite into theirs. It's a little suspenseful and super fun!

Cupcakes

Top your gender reveal cupcakes with pink and blue frosting, or have a surprise filling waiting on the inside.

Themed cookies

gender reveal cookies

Go long! A football-themed party can stay sweet thanks to these cookies featuring footballs and blue and pink jerseys.

Hot chocolate bar

gender reveal hot chocolate bar

Hosting a gender reveal party in the winter? Heat up some hot chocolate and offer a tray full of pink and blue toppings.

Chocolate-covered pretzel sticks

gender reveal pretzel sticks

A beloved savory-meets-sweet treat, these dipped pretzel sticks are an ideal finger food to munch on during your celebration.

What are some other gender reveal food ideas?

Not all your food needs to be color themed, of course. Turn to your favorite party foods, cravings, or ways to celebrate — do you prefer a brunch or a picnic, for example?

Community mom maverick1110 did a "baby-q" on July 3 for their reveal party, where they served BBQ foods and picnic-style sides, along with pink and blue cookies and punch.

Community member hannahmccuiston1234 says, "We did a simple brunch! I made pancakes that were pink and blue and just had eggs and bacon... It was really simple and stress free but very enjoyable!"

Here are some ideas for rounding out your party menu:

Pickles

Is your number one pregancy craving a big ol' dill spear? Share your favorite snack by offering pickles on a stick or a large platter of cukes. Add even more color to your party with other fresh veggies, too.

Yogurt parfaits

Guests will appreciate a healthful cup full of yogurt, fruit, and granola. This is a great option for a brunch-style celebration.

Popcorn cones

gender reveal popcorn cones

Filled with sweet kettle corn or salty kernels, pre-portioned cones make popcorn the cutest party snack. You can decorate the paper cups with a little message, too.

Salad

Hosting a light lunch or picnic-style gathering? Offer up some greens mixed with beans or chicken, your favorite vegetables, avocado, and a simple dressing.

Grazing boards

Arrange all of your favorite bites together on a beautiful board. Think: dried fruits, veggie spears, nuts, pretzels, grapes, and other pregnancy-safe snacks .

Ice cream

Another classic pregnancy craving, ice cream will thrill both kids and adults. Offer bowls of pink and blue candy to use as themed toppings.

"We had an ice cream themed party with a build-your-own sundae bar. It was so fun!" says Community mom Mearna.

What drinks can I serve at our gender reveal?

Guests will appreciate fun beverages, so don’t forget to give drinks some thought when you’re planning your party. As with foods, drinks can be color appropriate or party themed — or just a favorite way to make a toast.

Here are just a few ideas for keeping guests refreshed:

Blue and pink punch

gender reveal punch

Pre-pour beverages accessorized with pink and blue sugar-rimmed glasses.

Mom-osa

Offer a mocktail with juice and soda for the guest of honor, plus additional bubbly for others.

Fruit and veggie smoothies

Smoothies work well for daytime gatherings. Depending on the fruits and vegetables you use, you can also make your signature blend look blue or pink.

Your gender reveal party is a time to celebrate and prepare for your baby's arrival. By sweetening the deal with delectable snacks and treats, you and your guests can feel satisfied in every way. Cheers to you and your little boy or girl!

FAQ

Do you serve food at a gender reveal?

Yes, most hosts serve snacks and drinks at a gender reveal party. You can even incorporate food into the reveal itself!

What kind of food do you serve at a gender reveal?

Finger foods work well for gender reveal parties, especially pink and blue treats alongside classic party snacks.

What pink and blue foods can you serve at a gender reveal party?

Customize desserts like cupcakes, cookies, cake pops, and M&M's with your chosen colors for a gender reveal. And you can mix up a custom pink or blue punch for your guests to sip.

What desserts work well for a gender reveal?

If your gender reveal menu needs a little something sweet, you have a lot of options. Consider cake, cookies, cupcakes, cake pops, cheesecake bites, chocolate-covered pretzels, cotton candy, or even a hot chocolate or sundae bar for your party.
