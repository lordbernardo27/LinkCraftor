Meet the Winners of the 2025 What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards

What to Expect 2025 Mom Must-Have Awards seal on purple watercolor background

Shopping for a baby is, in a word, overwhelming. The sheer number of parenting and pregnancy products on the market (and how much it'll cost to get all that gear) alone is enough to send any new mom or dad into a shopping spiral. But when you find a product that truly makes life easier, whether that buys you five extra minutes of sleep or helps soothe your little one faster, it’s a win worth celebrating.

That’s exactly what the What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards are all about. This year’s winners are the products parents actually use, love, and recommend.

You know there are some non-negotiables, like a car seat and stroller , but others, while not crucial to your child’s survival, can feel just as essential as a new parent. Things like a baby carrier that gives your arms a break when your whole body is tired, or even a TikTok-viral product that actually lives up to the hype. (Heads up: Both are on the list below!)

How the Mom Must-Have Winners Were Chosen

No two babies (or parents) are the same. What works like magic for one family might flop for another. But when thousands of What to Expect parents agree on a singular favorite, you know it’s something special.

Our Mom Must-Have winners are chosen by the millions-strong What to Expect community, meaning every pick has been mom-tested and -approved. This year, more than 8,000 votes poured in across 20 categories, from maternity must-haves to baby gear and beyond. The products that rose to the top are the ones parents truly rely on day after day (and, yes, night after sleepless night). This way, new parents everywhere can shop with confidence (and maybe skip a few of those “oops, didn’t need that” purchases, too).

Without further ado, here are the winners of the 2025 What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards .

Best Pregnancy Pillow

Momcozy U-Shaped Pregnancy Pillow

Why We Love It

As your bump grows, your hips and back take on more pressure while you sleep, which can be uncomfortable and even painful. What to Expect moms swear by the Momcozy U-Shaped Pregnancy Pillow to help make pregnancy sleep a little easier.

Momcozy’s pillow, which was also a 2023 winner, has a U shape that hugs and gently supports your entire body, including your head, back, neck and feet. The pillow is so plush and the material so soft and cozy, that many moms say they continue to use it postpartum to provide support for nursing, sleeping or just sitting.

Good to know: There are a number of different color and fabric options, but if you tend to run hot, you’ll want to choose one that's made of cooling fabric .

Best Prenatal Vitamin

Nature Made Prenatal Multivitamin

Why We Love It

Packed with all the nutrients pregnant women need — like folate, vitamin D, iron and DHA — it’s no surprise that Nature Made’s Prenatal Multivitamin won this award for the fifth year in a row. But what our community members love most about these prenatal vitamins is how gentle they are on the stomach and taste buds. Many moms praised the softgel capsules for being easy to swallow, and free from both the chalky aftertaste of uncoated pills and the excess sugar of gummy vitamins. Nature Made is also USP-verified, a third-party certification that tests supplements for quality. As always, consult your doctor before adding any new supplement to your diet.

Good to know: The jars are available in a number of sizes, from 60, 90 or 150 softgels, with one softgel equaling a serving.

Best Baby Bouncer or Swing

Baby Björn Bouncer Balance Soft

Why We Love It

For many parents, the Baby Björn Bouncer Balance Soft is the ultimate soother, thanks to its natural rocking motion. The ergonomic design gives baby just enough support, and it can adjust to three different positions. If you need to take it on the go, it also folds flat and at just 4.6 pounds, it’s incredibly lightweight. At $210, it’s definitely an investment, but many parents say it’s well worth the price, especially when you consider that you can turn the fabric seat around to use it as a children’s chair.

The bouncer can accommodate babies up to 20 pounds, but you should retire it when baby starts trying to sit up. As a children’s chair, it can work for little ones up to 29 pounds. Plus, the seat cover is removable and machine-washable, so it can take on spit-up and blowouts (the brand recommends using a warm water setting).

Good to know: Though bouncers , swings and rockers are excellent for soothing fussy babies, little ones should never sleep in these products, as the inclined seat puts your baby at risk for suffocation. If she falls asleep in the Baby Björn Bouncer Balance Soft (or any other inclined product), move your baby to a safe sleep environment (such as a crib, bassinet or playard) right away.

Best Breast Pump

Spectra S1 Plus Electric Breast Pump

Why We Love It

Breast pumps have become increasingly high tech in the past few years, and there are more wireless and hands-free options than ever. But when it comes to sheer power and efficiency, What to Expect moms agree that the Spectra S1 Plus is still the very best electric breast pump on the market.

Spectra offers four different electric breast pumps: the S1 Plus , S2 Plus , S3 Pro and 9 Plus . But our community is particularly fond of the hospital-grade S1 Plus. (So much so that this is the third year it’s won this award!) Although the pump is compact and lightweight, it packs the same power as larger models, with some moms saying it helps them express twice the amount of milk as other pumps.

In addition to being powerful, moms love that the Spectra 1 is comfortable and relatively quiet. It’s also wireless, which is especially helpful for pumping moms who have to travel and aren’t always near an outlet.

Need something more hands-free? When it comes to wearable breast pumps , the Momcozy S12 Pro is a noteworthy runner-up. It features high-capacity cups (6 oz each), three expression modes, and nine intensity levels for a customizable, comfortable experience. With about 130 minutes of charging for up to 240 minutes of use, it’s a convenient, efficient option.

Good to know: The Spectra S1 features a nightlight and digital stopwatch, both of which can come in handy during late-night pumping sessions.

Best Formula Brand

Enfamil

Why We Love It

Enfamil offers an extensive line of baby formulas , but Enfamil NeuroPro seems to be a go-to for many families. The formula provides brain-building nutrients like DHA and immune-supporting prebiotics, ensuring your baby is getting the fuel they need to hit their milestones. Available as a powder or ready-to-feed formula, Enfamil NeuroPro is structurally similar to breast milk, which may make it easier for your baby to digest.

Be sure to consult with your pediatrician before starting your child on formula, as they may recommend finding one that addresses common feeding issues, such as colic or milk protein allergy. Fortunately Enfamil has options for both. Many What to Expect parents say the brand’s GentleEase formula was incredibly helpful for their little ones with sensitive stomachs.

Good to know: Enfamil’s container is dishwasher safe, so you can buy formula refills instead of a new tub every time, saving you money.

Best Baby Bottle Brand

Dr. Brown's

Why We Love It

Trusted by families for generations, Dr. Brown's bottles are known for making feeding and digesting easier. The line of Anti-Colic bottles feature a two-piece vent that keeps air from coming into contact with the milk. This prevents your baby from swallowing air, which can lead to spit-up, burping and gas. With the Options+ bottles, you can remove the vent as the baby's feeding progresses for a more age-appropriate bottle. (This can make them easier to clean, as the one complaint some parents have about Dr. Brown’s bottles is that they have many small parts.)

But the vent isn’t the only feature that makes Dr. Brown a favorite among community members. Many What to Expect parents note how their baby had little troubling transitioning from breast to Dr. Brown’s bottle and back. This is likely due to the bottle’s nipple, which is shaped like a breast to help reduce nipple confusion and provide a consistent flow of milk. Crucially, many parents also note that Dr. Brown’s bottles are secure and less likely to leak.

Good to know: Dr. Brown’s Anti-Colic Options+ are available as glass or plastic, and narrow- or wide-neck . The best one for you really comes down to personal preference. Wide bottles tend to be a little easier to clean, though narrow bottles, which are pictured here, are easier for babies to eventually hold on their own and also easier to fit in a cabinet.

Best Diaper Brand

Pampers

Why We Love It

You can never have too many diapers — or diaper options. With diapers, a lot comes down to fit, and what works for one baby might not be quite right for another. But What to Expect parents love Pampers for how consistently well-fitting and leak-proof they are.

Of the many options, Pampers Swaddler Diapers are a popular pick. The flexible bands on these diapers offer more stretch to accommodate your growing baby. The material is soft enough to be gentle on baby’s skin, but still strong enough to contain messes. In fact, Pampers claims its Swaddlers Diapers provide up to 12 hours of dryness protection. Additionally, the brand’s Dual Leak-Guard Barriers along the leg cuff further prevent leaks where they happen most. Many parents found these claims to be accurate, saying Pampers’ diapers are the best they’ve tried to prevent blowouts and leaks.

These diapers lack the patterns and designs of its competitors, but some parents like that, as the diaper doesn’t show through clothing.
