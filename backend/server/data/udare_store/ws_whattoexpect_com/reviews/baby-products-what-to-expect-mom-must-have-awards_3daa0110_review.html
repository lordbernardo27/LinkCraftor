Meet the Winners of the 2025 What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards

What to Expect 2025 Mom Must-Have Awards seal on purple watercolor background

Shopping for a baby is, in a word, overwhelming. The sheer number of parenting andpregnancy productson the market (andhow much it'll costto get all that gear) alone is enough to send any new mom or dad into a shopping spiral. But when you find a product that truly makes life easier, whether that buys you five extra minutes of sleep or helps soothe your little one faster, it’s a win worth celebrating.

That’s exactly what the What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards are all about. This year’s winners are the products parents actually use, love, and recommend.

You know there are some non-negotiables, like acar seatandstroller, but others, while not crucial to your child’s survival, can feel just as essential as a new parent. Things like ababy carrierthat gives your arms a break when your whole body is tired, or even a TikTok-viral product that actually lives up to the hype. (Heads up: Both are on the list below!)

How the Mom Must-Have Winners Were Chosen

No two babies (or parents) are the same. What works like magic for one family might flop for another. But when thousands of What to Expect parents agree on a singular favorite, you know it’s something special.

Our Mom Must-Have winners are chosen by the millions-strong What to Expect community, meaning every pick has been mom-tested and -approved. This year, more than 8,000 votes poured in across 20 categories, from maternity must-haves to baby gear and beyond. The products that rose to the top are the ones parents truly rely on day after day (and, yes, night after sleepless night). This way, new parents everywhere can shop with confidence (and maybe skip a few of those “oops, didn’t need that” purchases, too).

Without further ado, here are the winners of the2025 What to Expect Mom Must-Have Awards.

Kathleen Felton orginally contributed to this article.

Best Pregnancy Pillow

Momcozy U-Shaped Pregnancy Pillow

Why We Love It

As your bump grows, your hips and back take on more pressure while you sleep, which can be uncomfortable and even painful. What to Expect moms swear by theMomcozy U-Shaped Pregnancy Pillowto help make pregnancy sleep a little easier.

Momcozy’s pillow, which was also a 2023 winner, has a U shape that hugs and gently supports your entire body, including your head, back, neck and feet. The pillow is so plush and the material so soft and cozy, that many moms say they continue to use it postpartum to provide support for nursing, sleeping or just sitting.

Good to know: There are a number of different color and fabric options, but if you tend to run hot, you’ll want to choose one that'smade of cooling fabric.

Best Prenatal Vitamin

Nature Made Prenatal Multivitamin

Why We Love It

Packed with all the nutrients pregnant women need — like folate, vitamin D, iron and DHA — it’s no surprise thatNature Made’s Prenatal Multivitaminwon this award for the fifth year in a row. But what our community members love most about these prenatal vitamins is how gentle they are on the stomach and taste buds. Many moms praised the softgel capsules for being easy to swallow, and free from both the chalky aftertaste of uncoated pills and the excess sugar of gummy vitamins. Nature Made is also USP-verified, a third-party certification that tests supplements for quality. As always, consult your doctor before adding any new supplement to your diet.

Good to know: The jars are available in a number of sizes, from 60, 90 or 150 softgels, with one softgel equaling a serving.

Best Baby Bottle Brand

Dr. Brown's

Why We Love It

Trusted by families for generations, Dr. Brown's bottles are known for making feeding and digesting easier. The line ofAnti-Colic bottlesfeature a two-piece vent that keeps air from coming into contact with the milk. This prevents your baby from swallowing air, which can lead to spit-up, burping and gas. With the Options+ bottles, you can remove the vent as the baby's feeding progresses for a more age-appropriate bottle. (This can make them easier to clean, as the one complaint some parents have about Dr. Brown’s bottles is that they have many small parts.)

But the vent isn’t the only feature that makes Dr. Brown a favorite among community members. Many What to Expect parents note how their baby had little troubling transitioning from breast to Dr. Brown’s bottle and back. This is likely due to the bottle’s nipple, which is shaped like a breast to help reduce nipple confusion and provide a consistent flow of milk. Crucially, many parents also note that Dr. Brown’s bottles are secure and less likely to leak.

Good to know: Dr. Brown’s Anti-Colic Options+ are available asglassor plastic, and narrow- orwide-neck. The best one for you really comes down to personal preference. Wide bottles tend to be a little easier to clean, though narrow bottles, which are pictured here, are easier for babies to eventually hold on their own and also easier to fit in a cabinet.
