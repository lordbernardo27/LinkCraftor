About Micah Resnick, M.D., F.A.A.P.

Dr. Resnick is a board-certified pediatrician in Cincinnati, Ohio, with TriHealth.

After graduating from Brown University with a degree in Comparative Literature, Dr. Resnick studied medicine at Howard University in Washington, D.C. He then moved back to his hometown of New York City to complete his pediatric training at Children’s Hospital at Montefiore in the Bronx, New York.

Board certifications

Dr. Resnick is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and is a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Resnick is affiliated with Bethesda North Hospital and Good Samaritan Hospital.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Resnick is a member of the Ohio Chapter of the American Academy of Pediatrics. Most importantly, Dr. Resnick is a lucky husband and blessed father of three incredible kids — Lana, Eli and Sidney.
