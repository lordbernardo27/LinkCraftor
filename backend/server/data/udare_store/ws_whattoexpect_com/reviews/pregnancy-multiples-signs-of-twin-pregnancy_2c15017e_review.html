6 Signs You May Be Pregnant With Twins

Updates throughout to copy, information, formatting, and sources, plus new medical review.

twin pregnancy symptoms, pair of cherries

Think you may be pregnant with twins? Learn more twin pregnancy symptoms and risk factors often associated with carrying multiples.

Incredibly exhausted, like in a next-level kind of way? Feeling extremely queasy every time you see or smell food? It may be your body just reacting to pregnancy — but it also may be a sign that you have more than one bun in the oven.

Although there’s been a slight decrease in twin pregnancies over the last several years, there were still 114,483 twin births in 2022, the latest year for which stats are available, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Here are some things to know about twin pregnancies and twin pregnancy symptoms.

Key Takeaways

  • You might suspect you're having twins if your pregnancy symptoms feel extra intense — think: stronger morning sickness, major fatigue, and quicker weight gain — but you won't know for sure until that first ultrasound, usually between 6 and 9 weeks.
  • Higher-than-normal hCG levels or increased alpha-fetoprotein in your blood can be early hints of a twin pregnancy, especially if you've used fertility treatments or have a family history of twins.
  • Carrying twins can mean double the kicks, cravings, and yes, discomforts. But every twin pregnancy is different, and many women don't feel anything unusual until their doctor confirms it.

How soon can you tell you’re pregnant with twins?

Your doctor won’t be able to tell for sure until yourfirst trimester ultrasound, which is usually done between6 weeksand9 weeksof pregnancy but can be done any time beforeweek 14.

It is thought that you might be able to tell that you’re having twins as early as5 weeksif you havehigher than normal human chorionic gonadotropin hormone (hCG) levels.

Your body begins to makehCGas soon as theembryo implantsin the uterus. Levels surge those first few weeks of pregnancy, and they usually double every two to three days for about 10 weeks.

If your doctor orders a blood test to check hCG levels and yours are sky-high, it may be a sign that you have two (or more!) babies on board.

Again, that’s not always the case, and you won’t know for sure until that first ultrasound. Keep in mind, though, that there’s sometimes a downside to knowing so soon.

Doctors can now identify twin pregnancies earlier than ever, but about 20 to 30% of the time, you end uplosing a twinlater in the first trimester — a phenomenon known asvanishing twin syndrome. When this happens, the tissue of the miscarried twin is usually reabsorbed by your body.

Documentation of vanishing twin syndrome has grown significantly over the past few decades, as early ultrasounds have become routine. There are also more reports of vanishing twin syndrome in women older than 30, though that may be linked to the fact that moms over the age of 40 have higher rates of multiple pregnancies, especially with the use of fertility treatments.

The good news is that when vanishing twin syndrome occurs in the first trimester, a mom usually goes on to experience a normal pregnancy and delivers a single healthy baby with no complications.

How do doctors confirm twin pregnancies?

Call it mother’s instinct, but you may have a sneaking suspicion that you’re carrying twins early in pregnancy. You won’t get a formal confirmation, however, until the doctor does an ultrasound in your first trimester.

This is usually done anytime before week 14 of pregnancy, although it may be done earlier, usually between weeks 6 and 9, if your doctor wants to date the pregnancy, or make sure the pregnancy is viable (this is usually more common when your pregnancy has involvedfertility treatments). Your doctor may also order an earlier ultrasound ifpregnancy bloodworkreveals the following:

  • Elevated hCG levels. They may be quite high in a multiple pregnancy and your practitioner may want to confirm whether you're carrying more than one baby as soon as possible.
  • High Alpha-fetoprotein. This is a protein released by your babies’ liver and found in your blood. High levels can indicate that more than one fetus is making the protein.

Twin pregnancy symptoms

Double the babies sometimes means that you’ll have double the pregnancy discomforts — but not always. Each woman may have slightly different twin pregnancy symptoms.

  • Moremorning sickness.Nausea and vomiting can be worse in a twin pregnancy, thanks to higher levels of the hCG hormone circulating in your system. You may notice that it starts earlier, and lasts longer. And severe nausea and vomiting, a condition known ashyperemesis gravidarum, is more common among moms carrying twins too.
  • Tummy troubles. Since there’s more crowding in the area, you can expect more strain on your GI tract. This can lead to common pregnancy digestive discomforts likeheartburn, indigestion andconstipation.
  • Intensefatigue. No wonder you feel wiped out: Your body is working twice as hard to grow two babies. Since you’re probably also dragging around more weight, you’re also more likely to drag in general. Pregnancy symptoms like nausea or indigestion can also make it harder to sleep. And yes, if you’re expecting twins, you can also expect that it will thicken the normal brain fog of pregnancy.
  • Moreweight gainearlier. Experts advise a normal-weight mom expecting twins to gain between 37 and 54 pounds. That means you’ll be gaining about a pound a week in the first trimester alone.
  • Increased appetite. You’ll probably notice anincreased appetiteto help encourage that extra weight gain, although some women struggle with such bad morning sickness it’s hard for them to get the calories they need.
  • Morefetal movementsometimes in different areas of your tummy at once. While most pregnant women feel at some point that they’re carrying an octopus (or a professional boxer), you’ve now got four arms and four legs to pack plenty of punches and kicks. And sometimes you'll feel the babies moving at the same time but in different parts of your belly.

By the way, you can track all your symptoms — from strange cravings to weird twinges — and know when to call your doc with theWhat to Expectapp.

In addition, if you fit into any of the following categories, you’re more likely to be seeing double with fraternal twins (not identical) at your first ultrasound:

  • You’re over age 35. Thanks to higher levels of the reproductive hormone FSH, you’re more likely to release two eggs when you ovulate.
  • You’ve had fertility treatments. Any type of treatment that stimulates ovulation or implants more than one embryo raisesyour odds of fraternal twins.
  • You’re overweight or obese. If you have a BMI of over 30 when you conceive, you’re more likely to give birth to fraternal twins.
  • You’re Black. Fraternal twins are slightly more common among Black women and less common among Hispanics and Asians.
  • You have a family history. If fraternal twins are already in your family, you have a greater chance of having them yourself.

While there’s no magic crystal ball to tell you that you’re having twins, you can rely on a few key signs — and the knowledge that you’ll learn definitively whether you're carrying two babies by the end of your first trimester.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I'm pregnant with twins?

Some twin pregnancies increase yourpregnancy symptoms, leading to more severe nausea or fatigue, but the only way to know for sure that you're having twins is by having an ultrasound.

What are the signs of a twin pregnancy?

The signs of a twin pregnancy are different for everyone, but typically include heightened normal pregnancy symptoms. Common signs include moresevere nausea, earlier weight gain, intense fatigue and increased appetite.

Can a twin pregnancy be detected early on?

Yes. A twin pregnancy can often be detected in your first trimester by anultrasoundat 6 weeks or later. If your healthcare provider checks your hcG levels, a higher-than-normal level can be a sign of a twin pregnancy. But you'll need an ultrasound to confirm that it's really twins.
