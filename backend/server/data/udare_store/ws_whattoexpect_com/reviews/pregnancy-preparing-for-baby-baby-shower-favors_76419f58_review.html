Baby showers are so special: They’re a wonderful way to welcome future moms and dads into parenthood. They’re also a great opportunity to shower the parents-to-be with love and gifts to make their lives brighter and easier.

As a caring host, there are a few things you should remember to make it a party they won’t forget. You want your guests to have delicious food and something to keep everyone entertained, but you may also want to consider a thank-you gift that will make them feel special long after the shower is over.

Do you need to give guests baby shower favors?

While baby shower favors can be a great add-on to make your guests feel special, plenty of parents don’t opt for favors and that’s totally fine. For instance, while some moms in the What to Expect community say they’ve always taken home a small goodie after a baby shower, plenty of others have never. There areno hard-and-fast ruleswhen it comes to baby shower favors, so feel free to do what you’re most comfortable with, whether that’s giving guests a gift to remember the occasion by or just focusing on the event itself without any favors.

Baby shower favor ideas

If you do choose to give loved ones a parting gift, there are plenty of creative and reasonable baby shower favor ideas out there to send your guests home smiling.

Whether you’re looking to DIY, add a personalized touch or keep things budget-friendly, there are so many options — and all are special. Here are a few baby shower favor ideas to consider:

  • Food is always a hit. Cookies, cupcakes, bags of popcorn, chocolates or candy are all great, but keep in mind the dietary restrictions of your guests and consider options that everyone can enjoy.
  • DIY options keep it cost effective. Think body scrubs, handmade soap, bouquets of flowers from the garden or mason jar candles.
  • Personalized options work great for a smaller crowd. Costs can skyrocket quickly with personalized options, which is why custom gifts are generally better for more intimate events.
  • Self-care items are always nice. Things like candles, face masks, lip balms or body butter are great ideas, for instance. Include a mix of scent options so everyone can go home with something they love.

No matter which idea you go with, your baby shower favors are sure to make a great addition to the celebration.

We searched through the What to Expect community of millions of moms to hear what several other parents and parents-to-be had at their baby showers and what their guests loved.
