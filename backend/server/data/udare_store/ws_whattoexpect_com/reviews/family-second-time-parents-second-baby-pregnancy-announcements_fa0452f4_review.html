10 Best Second Pregnancy Announcements to Celebrate Baby Number Two (or Three!)

Seeing those two lines a second time around feels a little different — after all, this isn't your first rodeo. You've likely tackled countless diaper changes and braved asleep regressionor two, and now your family is expanding again!

From adorable personalized sweaters announcing a big sibling's newfound "promotion" to quirky Star Wars-themed digital templates, thesesecond-time pregnancyannouncements will help your nearest and dearest share in the joy.

1. Smocked big sis

smocked big sis

It doesn't get much cuter than this photo of big sister-to-be Cooper smiling with a sonogram showing her two (yes, two!) little brothers. We found a similar white and blue smocked bishop dresshereif you want to copy her adorable ensemble.

2. Big brother loading …

b is for big brother

Is the big bro-to-belearning his ABCs? Let him and your loved ones know that "B" is for big brother withthis T-shirtfrom Etsy.

3. Life's a movie

life is a movie

Talk about main character energy.This customizable movie posteris a digital download that you can print or post to your social media accounts. Customize your names and all those little "production" details like the release date and ratings!

4. For the grandparents-to-be

announcement mug

Before you tell the world about baby number two, you'll need totell your parentsthat they're becoming grandparents — again! Hand them coffee inthese personalized mugsthat say "Nana (or their own grandparent name) again, EST. 2024" the next time they stop by, and make sure you capture their reactions.

5. Big sis bliss

big sis bliss

We simply can't get enough of this hand-embroidered sweater trend, and you can order one to announce a big sibling's new title!This onethat says "Big Sis" in pink, purple and yellow floral writing looks very similar to the one shown here.

6. Grogu #2

Grogu announcement

Digitalpregnancy announcement templatesgive you the opportunity to share a curated, artistic flat-lay with just a few clicks.This Star Wars-inspired one, complete with a letter board stating "A Second Baby We Are Having" and various Star Wars baby items, is perfect for your Baby Yoda on the way.

7. Here we grow again

here we grow again

Puns and pregnancy go together like peanut butter and jelly — or in this case, gardens and overalls. We love this family portrait with a letterboard that says "Here We Grow Again," taken by@bayleerexphotography.

8. Expect the unexpected

expect the unexpected announcement

Oops … you did it again! This isanother digital templatethat details your biggest surprise yet. The flat lay features a letterboard that says "Surprise! We're due with baby #2" and a personalized onesie with sparkling lights as the background.

9. Dear Daddy

dear daddy announcement

If you're bursting with excitement to tell your partner, consider holding off by orderingthis surprise-in-a-boxfor Dad. The box includes an adorable onesie that says "Ready for Round 2?" and a little card that says "Daddy, we haven't met yet but I already know that I love you." And don't worry about keeping the secret for too long — it ships the day after you order.

You're about to be a mama times two, so you can celebrate accordingly by sportingthis T-shirtthat reads "Mama²" in your next Instagram post. Bonus points if you and your family are holding up your sonogram in the photo!
