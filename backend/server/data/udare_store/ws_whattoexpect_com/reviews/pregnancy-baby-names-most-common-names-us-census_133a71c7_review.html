Will You Find Your Baby’s Name on a Keychain? The Census Just Revealed the Most Common Names in America

Most common names in the US from the 2020 Census, small wooden peg dolls connected with twine

Among all living people in the country (including adults), these are the names that you’ll encounter most often.

If you’re considering naming your baby Michael, you’re not alone. In fact, Michael is officially the most common first name in the country, according todata from the U.S. Census Bureau.

The agency recently released a report that shows a total of 3,476,721 people in the U.S. share this name as of the 2020 Census. The next most common name for males is John, and after that, James.

As for female names, the most common is Mary, with 1,768,343 women having this biblical name. Following Mary, the most common names for girls are Maria, then Jennifer.

The Census name report differs from the usual “most popular names” list that theSocial Security Administration publisheseach year. Instead of counting the number of babies born with the same name each year, the Census collects the first names of every person living in the United States once every 10 years. Their list ranks the most commonly used names, across all birth years.

Looking at the breakdown of the 1,000 most commonly used names below, you’ll see more girl names than boy names. The reason: “The female population has much more first-name diversity than the male population — 16% of men have one of the 10 most frequent names among men, while only 7.8% of women have one of the top 10 names,"the Census reportstates.

Don’t let popularity stop you from choosing one of these common names for your own baby — it’s just a guarantee they’ll be able to find their name on a souvenir keychain!

Methodology

This data is based on first names provided by 2020 Census respondents and limited to the 1,000 most common names as reported by respondents. Names were assigned a sex based on the share of males and females with the name. Then names were ranked according to how many people altogether (of both sexes) have the name, according to the 2020 Census.
