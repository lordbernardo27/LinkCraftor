Curious What Your Baby Will Be Like? Start With Her Zodiac Sign

Updates to copy, information, sources, and formatting

Colorful baby teething toys shaped like zodiac symbols and animals

While the stars can't predict your baby's future, many parents enjoy exploring astrology as a fun way to think about their child's personality.

Based on your baby's birth chart — a snapshot of the sky at the exact moment she was born — her zodiac sign can offer playful insights into her personality. Will your baby be quiet, emotionally intelligent, and magnetic? Or maybe rambunctious, upbeat, and fiery? Astrology can be a fun way to celebrate the unique little person you're getting to know.

As you read on, you'll learn what each of the 12 zodiac signs says about your baby's personality — now and as they grow — along with what it means if that sign appears as your baby's moon or rising sign.

Key Takeaways

  • Astrology can offer insight into your baby's unique personality. A baby's natal birth chart — including her sun, moon, and rising signs — is a fun way to explore potential personality traits, emotional tendencies, and how your child may interact with the world.
  • Each zodiac sign is associated with distinct strengths and characteristics. From Aries' bold independence to Pisces' deep empathy, each of the 12 zodiac signs may influence a child's temperament, interests, communication style, and approach to relationships.
  • Understanding your baby's astrological profile may help guide parenting. Learning about your child's zodiac traits can help you better support your child's emotional needs, encourage her strengths, and appreciate her individuality as she grows.

How does astrology work?

Each child has her own unique, complex astrological birth chart, which is basically a snapshot depicting the location of every celestial body in the solar system at the time of a baby's birth.

In addition to planet placements, the birth chart includes your baby's:

  • Sun sign . This is a good place to start. It's the zodiac sign that the sun was moving through on your baby's birthday, which is believed to color her self-image, natural strengths, and identity.
  • Moon sign . A reflection of your child's emotional personality, or inner world, the moon sign can tell you how she processes feelings, what makes her feel safe, and her emotional needs.
  • Rising (Ascendant) sign . Often described as the "face" the world sees, the rising sign reflects how others perceive your child, her approach to new experiences, and her social style.

You can find all these astrological placements (and more) bycasting a birth chartor using ourBirth Chart Calculator, and log your baby's milestones in the My Journal section of the freeWhat to Expect app.

"I love the little hints into our tiny babies that astrology can bring," saysWhat to Expect Communitymember katievith.

What are the traits of an Aries baby (March 21 – April 20)?

aries sign of the zodiac symbol

Your little one, born under the cardinal fire sign Aries and symbolized by the Ram, is destined to be an independent go-getter. That's because Aries is ruled by Mars, the crimson planet that, in astrology, governs how we take action and express our energy.

Mars' influence means your child will be fired up to get moving ASAP — and preferably at a lightning speed. Whether you're out in a jogging stroller or preparing her next meal, there's no such thing as too fast for a restless, energized Aries.

"My first daughter is an Aries," says Community mom nin3s. "She's a deep-feeling, compassionate firecracker. She's super energetic, impulsive, assertive, independent, and bold. Has been since day one."

That said, moving too slowly might fuel a red-facedtemper tantrum. In other words, even more than with the average eager tot, you may find yourself teaching your tiny Aries the value of patience.

Because Mars also rules courage, athleticism, and assertiveness, your mini Ram is likely to be a big fan of physical activity. She'll be gleeful about any chance to win or come out on top, whether that's crossing a "finish line" you set up with blocks or hearing applause aftertummy time.

You might find your little Aries especially motivated by competition and the possibility of being declared "the winner" of any activity. Once she'slearned to walk, it won't be long before she wants torun! Toddlerhood could find your rambunctious, impulsive kiddo zipping around the playground, excited to learn to ride a tricycle orplay catch.

As the first of 12 signs and the one associated with the First House of Self, Aries is linked to independence, self-confidence, and initiative. Your little Ram may grow up with a strong sense of self and a natural drive to forge her own path. As a cardinal sign, she's also inclined to take the lead and think in a big-picture, visionary way.

At the same time, Aries is often considered the "baby" of the zodiac, so your little one may exude playfulness and wide-eyed wonder well beyond childhood.

What it means if your baby's moon sign is Aries: Your Aries moon baby may be quick-tempered but then refocus her attention on the next thing just as speedily. She'll likely find comfort and security in fast-paced, high-energy, playful activities.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Aries: Your Aries rising baby may come across as self-sufficient and capable of taking charge of any situation. She'll likely be perceived as athletic, dynamic, and direct.

What are the traits of a Taurus baby (April 21 – May 20)?

taurus sign of the zodiac symbol

Born under the fixed earth sign Taurus, symbolized by the Bull, your little one is down-to-earth and deeply connected to the world around her. She experiences life through all five senses — touch, taste, smell, sound, and sight. These characteristics are often linked to Taurus' ruling planet, Venus, which in astrology governs beauty, art, relationships, and values.

Venus' influence means your mini Bull revels in life's simple pleasures, whether that's snuggling into a super softswaddleblanket or enjoying a leisurely stroll through nature. Spending time outdoors may be particularly soothing for her. And because Taurus is highly tactile,skin-to-skin timemay become a favorite bonding experience for both of you.

As your Taurus grows, you can tap into her Venusian love of beauty and creativity through activities like finger painting, crafting, or listening to music (perhaps especially songs with a heartfelt message, like "Skidamarink" or "My Favorite Things" from The Sound of Music ). As an Earth sign, she may also show an early appreciation for nature, making family gardening projects a natural fit.

But no matter what activity you're enjoying together, keep in mind that even the tiniest Taurus is happiest when she can move at her own pace — typically slow, steady, and deliberate. If she's pushed to keep up with a faster or more hectic lifestyle, you could be facing a downpour of tears.

As one of the fixed signs, Taurus is a creature of habit, known for lovingroutines, familiarity, and the comforts of home. Consistency helps your little Bull feel secure and grounded.

Because Taurus is associated with the Second House of Material Possessions, your baby may become especiallyattached to objects, like a special lovey. The Second House is also linked to self-worth, so your Taurus may grow into someone with a strong sense of her own value. When she makes friends with people she feels in sync with, she'll likely be endlessly loyal and reliable.

Of course, Taurus' fixed-sign nature can also translate to a stubborn streak. Your little one may find it harder than some kids toadapt to change, so it can help to gently encourage flexibility and resilience when things don't go according to plan.

"Our two boys are Scorpio and Taurus so you can imagine there's a lot of attitudes and stubbornness here," says Community mom wacisej.

What it means if your baby's moon sign is Taurus: Your Taurus moon baby feels most nurtured when she's engaging in routines and practices that are familiar — think hearing the same "good morning" song daily upon waking or enjoying a predictable rotation of yummy snacks. Her connection to the five senses also means she'll feel more secure surrounded by familiar scents, sounds, sights, and textures.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Taurus: Your Taurus rising baby may come off as peaceful, steadfast, determined, and perhaps a bit cautious. Others are likely to see her as someone who takes her time and develops strong preferences that she doesn't easily abandon.

"I don't even know yet if my baby is a boy or a girl but somehow I'm more curious about the zodiac sign," says Community momnihaomatilda.

What are the traits of a Gemini baby (May 21 – June 21)?

gemini sign of the zodiac symbol

Your little one, born under the mutable air sign Gemini and symbolized by the Twins, is:

  • Communicative
  • Social
  • Curious
  • Vivacious
  • Highly motivated to zip from one activity to the next on a hunt for more mental stimulation

Her nature is influenced by her ruling planet, Mercury, the "messenger planet," which governs communication, transportation, and technology in astrology. (Mercury also rules Virgo.)

Mercury's influence results in Gemini being one of the signs most eager to learn and soak up new information. Thirsty to connect with everyone from you or a sibling to a grandparent or a neighbor, little Geminis are happiest when they're out in the world, observing and engaging with people. A natural communicator, your little one might "chit-chat" with you early on, even if that's just through coos or giggles.

And Mercury's energy is just as much about listening as it is about talking, so you may notice that your tiny Twin is especially interested in taking in a variety of sounds — from a bird's song at the park to your BFF's laugh. You could be astounded by her flawless mimicry!

As she moves into toddlerhood, your little Gemini will likely become even more playful, friendly, and wide-eyed with wonder. She may channel this energy into a wide range of interests, many of them connected to language, learning, and technology — all Mercury's terrain.

For example, as much as your Gemini baby loves diving into one book after the next, she will also adore making up stories with you at bedtime. Interactive games, educational apps, and engaging TV shows could hold special appeal as well. In general, any opportunity she has to take in new information will excite your tiny Gemini.

And as one of the mutable signs, she'll also appreciate the freedom to flit from one thing to the next. You might find it helpful to try new "baby and me" classes, visit different playgrounds, or regularly introduce her to new foods and experiences. Your mercurial Gemini relishes change and variety.

As the sign associated with the Third House of Communication, which also governs siblings and short-distance travel, your Gemini baby is linked to connection and exploration. Your little Twin may delight in opportunities to connect with others and discover new places, whether that's aplaydateor a quick trip to the grocery store with you.

What it means if your baby's moon sign is Gemini: Your Gemini moon baby is likely to feel most comforted by interaction, conversation, and learning activities. Her emotional well-being is closely tied to her curious, intellectual nature, so talking through big feelings and emotionally challenging moments can help her feel understood and supported.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Gemini: Your Gemini rising baby may come across as quick-witted, energetic, high-spirited, friendly, and fun-loving. Her enthusiasm for a wide range of activities will make her a magnet for a wide social circle.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Cancer: Your Cancer rising baby may be perceived as sensitive, sweet, and somewhat self-protective. Others are likely to see her as thoughtful, compassionate, and eager to care for everyone from her friends to her pets.

Her charisma and innate joyfulness tend to endear her to others, and you'll get the sense that your regal Lion positively soaks up the limelight.

Because Leo is associated with the Fifth House of Self-Expression, your baby Lion is naturally imaginative and drawn to theatrical experiences, whether that's putting on a play or initiating adance party. She enjoys finding her voice and, through her confidence and pride in who she is, may even inspire friends and family to do the same.

What it means if your baby's moon sign is Leo: Your Leo moon baby is likely comfortable roaring about her feelings — both positive and negative. She'll feel most cared for when engaging with loved ones through spirited, playful activities, such as pretending to be characters from a favorite movie.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Leo: Your Leo rising baby may appear confident, driven, sunny, and self-aware. Whether she ultimately dreams of being a lawmaker or a TV star, she'll likely possess a natural flair for commanding attention.

virgo sign of the zodiac symbol

  • Thoughtful
  • Detail-oriented
  • Helpful
  • Highly cerebral

These traits are rooted in the fact that Virgo is one of two signs ruled by Mercury, the planet of communication, technology, and transportation. (The other is Gemini.)

Thanks to Mercury's influence, your little Maiden has a busy, information-gathering mind from the time she's tiny, taking in even the most minute details of any given situation. She's also quite perceptive, picking up on subtle shifts in your mood or changes in the tone of your voice.

As she gets older, she'll likely want to channel all of this intel into being helpful to you and others. Few things make a Virgo toddler light up more than knowing she's learned something that benefits someone she cares about.

For example, she may take pride in knowing that brushing her own teeth gives you more time to get a younger sibling ready in the morning. And given her innate knack for organization, she'll likely enjoy helping with tidying projects around the house.

Mercury's influence is also responsible for Virgo's love of language, reading, learning, and research. This may initially show up as an endless stream of curious questions — perhaps even more than the average child asks. She has the potential to become a real bookworm, so prioritizing story time together can support both her intellectual and emotional development, especially because she'll cherish the one-on-one connection.

As they grow, many Virgos discover that telling stories, writing, or journaling can be both joyful and therapeutic.

As one of the mutable signs, Virgo is innately adaptable, flexible, and capable of going with the flow. Sure, she may be particular about certain details — perhaps fussing if she's fed in a specific position or asked to wear a scratchy outfit to a formal event — but for the most part, she's up for following your lead.

That said, the drawback of mutability is that your tiny Maiden can struggle with indecision — usually because she worries she'll make the "wrong" choice. Encouraging her to trust herself, listen to her inner voice, and apply the information she's gathered to make thoughtful decisions can help build confidence over time.

As the sign associated with the Sixth House of Wellness and Daily Routine, Virgo is linked to healthy habits and self-care. Even as a child, your little Virgo may appreciate routines that help her feel grounded. Because her analytical nature can sometimes tip into worry, age-appropriate stress-management tools — such as breathing exercises or kid-friendly yoga — may prove especially helpful.

She also has a natural appreciation for all the seemingly minor to-do's that make up a daily schedule, so it may be easy — and fun — to involve her inchoreslike gardening, grocery shopping, or meal prep.

What it means if your baby's moon sign is Virgo: Your Virgo moon baby may feel most comforted by intellectual connection and praise for her helpfulness. Because she can sometimes struggle to distinguish between what her heart feels and what her head thinks, it may help to encourage both perspectives from an early age by asking, "How do you feel?" as well as "What do you think?"

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Virgo: Your Virgo rising baby may appear communicative, pragmatic, and eager to lend a hand. Others are likely to see her as someone who enjoys helping friends and loved ones whenever possible.

What it means if your baby's rising sign/ascendant is Libra: Your Libra rising baby may come across as stylish, peace-seeking, and eager to make the world around her as beautiful as possible.
