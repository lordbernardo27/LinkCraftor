About Robin Schaffran, M.D.

Dr. Robin Schaffran is a board-certified dermatologist practicing both pediatric and adult dermatology in Beverly Hills, California.

Dr. Schaffran completed her dermatology residency at Oregon Health & Science University in Portland, Oregon, and an internal medicine internship at Harvard Medical School’s Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center in Boston, Massachusetts.

She received her M.D. from the University of Toronto, where she graduated as a member of the Alpha Omega Alpha honors society. She previously earned a Bachelor of Science from McGill University in Montreal.

Board certification

Dr. Schaffran has been board-certified with the American Board of Dermatology since 1999.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Schaffran is affiliated with Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles, California.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Schaffran is a fellow of the American Academy of Dermatology and a member of the Women’s Dermatologic Society and the American Society for Laser Medicine and Surgery.

She is married and a mom to three wonderful daughters ages 13, 12 and 9. She enjoys traveling, hiking, running, cooking and spending time with family.

Publications and research

Dr. Schaffran received a clinical research fellowship grant from the American Contact Dermatitis Society in 1997. She has been published in several peer-reviewed journals.
