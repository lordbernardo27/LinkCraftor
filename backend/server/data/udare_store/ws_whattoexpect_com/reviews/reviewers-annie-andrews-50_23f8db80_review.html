About Annie Andrews, M.D.

Dr. Annie Andrews is a board-certified pediatrician and a Clinical Professor of Pediatrics at George Washington School of Medicine.

Experience

Dr. Andrews has a B.S. degree in pre-medicine from the University of Dayton. She earned her M.D. from University of Cincinnati College of Medicine and completed her residency in pediatrics at Cincinnati Children's Hospital.

Board certifications

Dr. Andrews is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Andrews is affiliated with Children's National Medical Center in Washington, D.C..

Other relevant positions

Dr. Andrews is a faculty member at George Washington School of Medicine and a fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics (FAAP). She is a Senior Advisor at Everytown for Gun Safety and Moms Demand Action. She is the Founder and CEO of Their Future. Our Vote. and Their Future PAC.

Publications

Dr. Andrews has published nearly 100 peer-reviewed publications in medical journals ranging in focus from asthma to pediatric firearm injuries.

Awards

Dr. Andrews has received multiple awards from the AAP and the American Medical Association for her work in child advocacy.
