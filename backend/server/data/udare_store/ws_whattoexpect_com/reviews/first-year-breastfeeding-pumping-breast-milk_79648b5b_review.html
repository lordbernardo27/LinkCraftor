Pumping 101

Pumping might seem daunting at first (tubes and flanges and suction, oh my!), but it’s not complicated once you get the knack of it. Here's everything new parents need to know about pumping breast milk.

All About Pumping

How to feed your baby is a personal choice that’s different for every family, and there’s no “right” way to nourish your little one. Many moms pump as part of their breastfeeding journey (such as in combination with nursing or formula), and some othersexclusively pump.

As with breastfeeding, pumping requires strength and discipline, and it’s incredibly time-intensive. If you’re a mom who pumps, know that you’re not alone. Consider this your complete guide to pumping breast milk, whether you're pumping exclusively,pumping when you return to workor pumping occasionally.

Everything you need to know to start your pumping journey, from how to get a breast pump through health insurance to choosing the right size flange for your nipples.

Exclusive Pumping

Exclusive pumping (also referred to as “EPing”) means that you make the choice to give your baby only breast milk that you express or pump. While many moms find it rewarding to be able to feed their babies breast milk they've expressed, EPing is not without its challenges. Here's what to know if you're planning on pumping exclusively.
