Why Nuuly Is Worth It For Maternity Clothes

A legit maternity section with actually stylish pieces

I tried several clothing subscriptions pre-kids, and they all had one thing in common: an abysmal maternity section. The options were slim and, frankly, dated. NotNuuly.

For $98 a month, you can rent six pieces at a time and swap them out each month. Its virtual closet holds more than 300 maternity pieces (yes, even jeans) designed to be worn with a growing bump. You’ll find baby shower dresses, office-ready trousers, and effortlessly cool matching sets from brands likeIngrid & Isabel,Hatch,Emilia George, and more.

It can cost less than buying a temporary wardrobe

$98 a month is not insignificant. But maternity denim alone can cost $150 or more. Add a few dresses, work staples, and event outfits, and you can easily spend several hundred dollars on clothes you will wear for a single season.

If you use Nuuly for four or five months during peak bump growth, the total can be comparable to buying a short-term wardrobe outright. The difference is you do not have to store or resell anything afterward.

Plus, there’s no need to store bins of maternity clothes or figure out how to resell them later. When you’re done, you simply send everything back using the pre-paid label.

It works postpartum, too

We do not talk enough about postpartum clothes because they barely exist as a category. You’re either still wearing maternity pieces that now feel too loose, squeezing into pre-pregnancy clothes that do not fit comfortably yet, or buying new items that may not fit in a few weeks.

Nuuly offers a fourth option: renting clothes that fit your body right now.

Most pieces are available in a wide size range (typically 00 to 16), making it easy to size up or down as your body continues to change. And because the selection rotates seasonally, you’re not stuck trying to make a linen dress work in 30-degree weather.

It’s a flexible, low-commitment way to feel put-together during a phase when getting dressed can feel surprisingly hard.

Is Nuuly Right for You?

If you’re pregnant, attending events, or commuting to an office and don’t want to build a temporary wardrobe from scratch,Nuulyis definitely worth considering.

The $98 monthly fee may not make sense if you only need a pair of jeans and a few tees. But if you have multiple social plans, work events, or special occasions, renting can be the more flexible option.

As one What to Expect community member put it: “Given how fast the body changes, renting clothes feels like the more sustainable and affordable route than purchasing a bunch of new clothes.”
