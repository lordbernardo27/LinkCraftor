What is a gender reveal cake?

Cut and served at agender revealparty, this delicious treat comes with an answer inside: boy or girl?

Ahead of time, you might undergo throughnoninvasive prenatal testing(NIPT), chorionic villus sampling (CVS) or amniocentesis at your doctor’s recommendation as early as 10 weeks into your pregnancy. These determine biological sex based on chromosomes tested in the mother's blood: XX is considered female and XY is considered male. Alternatively, you may wait until theanatomy ultrasound, when a technician or doctor determines the sex based on fetal genitalia development.

At your appointment, let your doctor know that you want to learn your baby's sex later on as a surprise. Ask the practitioner to stow away the results in an envelope. From there, you can hand it to your local baker, or give it to the party host to submit the order. Just try not to peek!

Gender reveal cakes typically feature a combination of blue and pink, but you can choose any color of the rainbow, and adorn your cake with adorable critters or fun symbols. Parents will often cut into gender reveal cakes, and see pink- or blue-dyed cake or frosting as an indicator of the news.

This idea also works well withgender reveal cupcakes. You can even add blue or pink dye to just one of them, pass out to friends and family, and then see which loved one has the answer!

FAQ

How do gender reveal cakes work?

Parents will often cut into a gender reveal cake as a way to find out whether they're having baby boy or baby girl. Inside, they'll see pink- or blue-dyed cake or frosting.

What can I make for a gender reveal party?

In lieu of a gender reveal cake, you can make gender reveal cake pops or cupcakes as a sweet way to share the surprise.

Do you have cake at a gender reveal party?

It's not required, but many parents choose to include a gender reveal cake as part of their party plans. It's a fun way to both break the news and serve dessert to your guests.

How much cake do I need?

An 8-inch round cake will serve about 15 to 20 people, where as a 10-inch round cake will feed about 25 to 40 guests.
