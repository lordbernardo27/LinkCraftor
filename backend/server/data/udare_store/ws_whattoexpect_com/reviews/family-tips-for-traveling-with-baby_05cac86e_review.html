5 Tips for Traveling with Baby

Traveling with a baby can be tough. Here are some mom-tested tips for surviving - and even enjoying - the journey!

1. Ditch the Stroller

"Wearing baby keeps him calm and is easier to maneuver through crowds." --AdventureKate

2. Reserve an airplane bassinet.

"The bassinet makes all the difference in the world! It literally was our lifesaver!" -- AnoshkaMKhan

3. Dress for Success

"Dress baby in comfy fleece pajamas since it gets pretty cold on the plane. Pack a change of clothes for both of you in case of any mishaps during your travels." -- MamaDesperado

4. Avoid Earaches

"Have him suck on something (paci/bottle/breast) during take-off and landing." -- vr1

5. Be Good to Fellow Travelers

"My baby likes to yell, so I'm making a double-sided 'sorry!' sign to raise as needed." --Soudie
