When It Comes to Diapers, What's on The Inside Matters

Kudos Diapers

Kudos replaces the typical plastic liner with cotton, which is gentler on sensitive skin.

Just so you know, What to Expect may earn commissions from the shopping links included on this page. All sales and prices are accurate at the time of publishing.

Before my daughter was born, I didn’t know much about diapers beyond the fact that we were going to need—well, a whole lot of them. I quickly learned that not all diapers are created equal, especially if you’re navigating the tricky world of blowouts and sensitive skin.

I found myself hunting for a diaper that could handle the heavy lifting of absorbency without turning her delicate, rash-prone skin red. One brand my birth group kept recommending wasKudos, which prioritizes absorbency without sacrificing baby's skin health.

What Makes Kudos Different

Most diapers focus heavily on absorbency, often using a plastic-based inner liner to get the job done. That works for many babies, but if you’re dealing with constant redness or irritation, you might need to rethink what’s sitting against their skin all day.

Kudos is the first diaper brand to swap that plastic layer for 100% cotton, which is naturally breathable to help reduce the moisture buildup and heat that cause rashes. But that doesn’t mean you sacrifice performance. Their patented DoubleDry™ system uses two absorbent layers to pull moisture away quickly, meaning they can hold their own during long naps or overnight stretches.

How Moms Rate Kudos

A cotton lining sounds lovely, but we know the real test happens during those long nights or busy afternoons.

Many What to Expect community members praise Kudos for containing even the biggest messes. One mom, who has been using Kudos for 14+ months, says, "They never leak or have blowouts, and I love how clean they are." Because they are totally chlorine-free and offer a convenient subscription plan, they’ve become a go-to for many parents looking for peace of mind.

Of course, every baby is unique! While many parents find them to be a dream, it’s worth noting that every little one has different needs. Trialing different brands and sizes is a common part of the parenting journey as you figure out what fits your baby best.

Kudos vs. Premium Diaper Brands

Price-wise, Kudos sits in that premium category — typically around $0.45 per diaper compared to $0.50 to $0.55 for some other premium brands, and higher than the $0.15 to $0.25 you might see for mainstream options. Ultimately, it comes down to the materis. Again, Kudos is one of the only diaper brands — premium or mainstream — that uses 100% cotton liners. You’re paying for that specific material difference, which can feel incredibly worth it if you’ve spent weeks rotating through rash creams.

Should You Switch to Kudos?

Kudosisn’t an "all-or-nothing" choice. Many families decide to use them just for nighttime to protect delicate skin during those long stretches of sleep, while using other options during the day. They might be a great fit for your family if:

  • Your baby has sensitive skin or eczema: That cotton layer can make a world of difference.
  • You're dealing with frequent diaper rash: It’s one of the only disposable options designed to change what touches the skin.
  • You prioritize "cleaner" materials: If you prefer plant-based fibers and chlorine-free materials, this is a wonderful option to explore.

Whether you find your "holy grail" diaper in a premium box or a mainstream brand, the most important thing is that you’re keeping baby comfortable and cared for. Take a deep breath, trust your gut, and don’t be afraid to try a few different options until you find the perfect fit for your little one.
