And yes, puppies count as older kids.

For seasoned parents, pregnancy might not be your first rodeo. But bringing baby number two (or three…or four!) into the mix is still a big deal. Your little one is about to gain a new sibling, and that’s going to shake up the family dynamics in exciting ways. It's the perfect moment to capture the joy with familymaternity photos, and don’t forget to include those sweet, eagerbig siblings!

Whether you’re drawn to the serene simplicity of natural settings or the timeless elegance of a studio, there are countless ways to create beautiful family photos. With the help of a skilled local photographer, all that’s left for your clan to do is smile and say, “Cheese!”
