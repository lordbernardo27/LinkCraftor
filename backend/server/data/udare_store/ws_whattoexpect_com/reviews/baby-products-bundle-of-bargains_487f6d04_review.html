Bundle of Bargains

Raising a child isn’t cheap — especially during the first year of life. But it doesn’t have to break the bank. Here's the the ultimate guide to finding deals on baby gear, from shopping tips to sales events.

Make Your Dollar Go Further

At What to Expect, we pride ourselves on helping you find the best baby gear that meets your family’s individual needs, lifestyle and, above all else, budget. Because, as we all know,babies are expensive. Fortunately, we aren’t just experts on shopping: We’re experts on saving, too. In addition to helping you sort through the endless supply of baby gear, What to Expect is also here to help stretch your dollar further by finding the best deals and money-saving tips.

To start, our weeklyBest Baby Dealsroundup highlights all the sales on on must-have parenting and baby gear from your favorite retailers, like Amazon, Target and more. We also scour major sales events, likePrime DayandBlack Friday, for deals that will cut costs on your parenting and baby essentials. Finally, we share money-saving tips that we’ve learned through our years of research and personal experience, like creating a Buy Buy Baby registry to save 15 percent on items. (More on that in a bit!)

Here, your ultimate guide to cutting costs on baby-related expenses, because even small discounts can add up to a big impact on your finances.
