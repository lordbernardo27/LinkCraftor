Is Your Child Ready for Preschool?

preschool

Ready, set…go? Take our quiz to find out if the timing is right for your tot to start preschool.

How can you predict whether it's time to load up your little one's backpack and pack him off to preschool? Skip the Magic 8 Balls and Ouija boards, and look no further than your child to clue you in. What your potential preschooler will be expected to know and be able to do before he's signed up will depend on the school you're considering as well as his age (preschool can start as early as age 2, or as late as age 4). But there are some general indications of readiness that can help you decide if preschool should be in your child's near future.

Grab a sheet of paper and a pen and take our yes-or-no quiz to find out if your tot has what it takes to make the grade — and pick up tips for upping his chances of preschool success.
