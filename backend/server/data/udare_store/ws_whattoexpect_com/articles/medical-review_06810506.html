Editorial Policies

References, sources and citations: What to Expect adheres to the highest standards of journalistic integrity, with sourcing guidelines relying only on peer-reviewed medical journals, academic institutions, government agencies and highly respected medical organizations such as theAmerican College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), theAmerican Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)and theCenters for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)as primary sources of information. You can see the sources we use in the “view sources” field at the bottom of every health article, along with the last reviewed date at the top.

Editorial independence: What to Expect’s editorial team maintains complete editorial independence and control over content. No unrelated partners or sponsors are permitted to change content or influence the selection of content (seeAdvertising Policy). Additionally, What to Expect participates in affiliate marketing programs, but this does not impact what products we choose to cover. We maintain a strictproduct review policy, and commissions earned simply help support all the work we do.

Contributors: What to Expect works with writers who specialize in creating content about pregnancy-, parenting- and health-related topics. All contributors are carefully vetted for their subject matter expertise, and are provided additional training in sourcing, researching and reporting.

Content updates: What to Expect adheres to a content maintenance schedule. Additionally, our editors update articles when new information becomes available. Articles may be updated when there are new clinical guidelines, changes in standards of care, new practice recommendations or product recalls. What to Expect editors also review articles when readers alert us about potential issues with our content (contact us at edit@whattoexpect.com ).

Links to other sites: What to Expect articles sometimes contain links to other websites. We neither control nor endorse these linked sites, nor does the company review or approve the content that appears on them. What to Expect is not responsible for the legality, accuracy or inappropriate nature of any content, advertising, products or other materials on or available from any linked sites.
