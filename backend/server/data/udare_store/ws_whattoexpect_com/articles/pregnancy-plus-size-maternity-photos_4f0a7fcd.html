These moms celebrate their bodies — and their babies on the way.

Pregnancy is a beautiful thing, but for many moms, it can bring up a lot of emotions about the changes their bodies go through along the way. As oneWhat to Expectmom recently said in the WTE Community, "I’m afraid I won’t be comfortable enough to take bump pics or have a maternity photo shoot."

What followed was an outpouring of support from plus-size parents and parents-to-be, sharing their own photos and encouraging words. As one wise mom advised, “I want to remind you your body is doing something amazing! You are growing life and that is beautiful…take the maternity photos.”

In the spirit of that sentiment, here are 14 beautiful pictures from different moms’ maternity shoots that serve as a gorgeous reminder to take the photos..
