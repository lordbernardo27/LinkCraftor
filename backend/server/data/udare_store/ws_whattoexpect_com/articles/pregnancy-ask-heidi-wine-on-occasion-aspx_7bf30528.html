Pregnant and Craving a Glass of Wine? Why It’s Best to Skip It (for Now)

by Heidi Murkoff , author of What to Expect When You're Expecting.

Updates to copy, sources, and formatting.

can pregnant women drink wine?

"What are your thoughts on the occasional glass of wine? I've heard conflicting stories. Is it okay to splurge once in a while, or is it better to play it safe?"

Unfortunately there's no research to support that an occasional glass of wine (or cocktail, or beer) is a safe bet when you're expecting — and ultimately, even a little wine while pregnant is just not worth the risk.

Key Takeaways

  • Zero alcohol is best. All the big health orgs (CDC, ACOG, AAP) say there's no safe level of booze during pregnancy — so it's wise to skip it during pregnancy.
  • The risks can vary. It's unknown whether a little or a lot of alcohol does damage to your unborn baby, which is why avoiding it is the best option.
  • Rock a mocktail. Yes, you can have a special drink! There are so many delicious alcohol-free drinks on the market and menus today that you're sure to find one you love.

The American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG), the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the Surgeon Generalall recommend abstaining from alcohol during pregnancy, including the occasional glass of wine, altogether.

Why? Mostly because nobody knows what the safe limit is — and depending on all kinds of variables, that safe limit may vary from woman to woman and fetus to fetus.

True, in many European countries an occasional glass of wine during pregnancy, sipped with food during a meal, is accepted. But given all of the risks of drinking when you're expecting, it's best to avoid it altogether. Although abstaining may seem like a high hurdle now, the next few dry months will fly by (and you will drink again!).

If you'd like an alternative to quench your thirst, try apregnancy-safe mocktail. A few refreshing options — some of which have the added benefit of nausea-calming ginger:

  • Ginger mule: Mix together non-alcoholic ginger beer, a few slices of cucumber, and a squeeze of fresh lime juice and pour over ice.
  • Faux mojito: Combine club soda, lime juice, mint leaves, and simple syrup together over ice.
  • Watermelon slushie: Mix watermelon, ice cubes, and honey in a blender.
  • Fake champagne: Combine one part pineapple juice, two parts white grape juice, and three parts ginger ale, to taste.
