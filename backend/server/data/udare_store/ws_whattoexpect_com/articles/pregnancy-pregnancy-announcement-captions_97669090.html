From short and sweet to long and sentimental, there are so many ways to share the big news on social media.

Whether you're after a quick one-liner or sentimental story, pregnancy announcement captions can come in every style — funny, sweet, or deeply personal. Below are options for every situation, from first pregnancies and multiples to rainbow babies and IVF journeys, plus seasonal ideas and quotes for extra inspiration.

After weeks and weeks of waiting to publicly share your adorable not-so-little secret, announcing your pregnancy can feel exhilarating. While some parents have been leaning into the trend of not sharing the news on social media andhard launching their babyafter they're born, others (myself included!) may be eager to post about their exciting news.

And if you’re still riding the emotional high (or nerves!) of seeing those two pink lines, download theWhat to Expect appto track early symptoms and connect with other expectant parents going through the same first-flutter feelings.

But what do you actually say when you post about your soon-to-be bundle of joy? Is your caption simple, sappy, sentimental, or sweet? "My timeline and newsfeed is FULL of pregnancy announcements," saysWhat to Expect Communitymom Milz5421. "I haven’t posted mine because I have no idea how I want to announce!"

Pregnancy announcement captions can be as simple as “We’re expecting!” or a more detailed explanation of the journey that led to those two little lines on the pregnancy test. Here's some caption inspiration to spark your creativity.

Key Takeaways

  • Pregnancy announcement captions may be short and punny or long and heartfelt — the tone is entirely up to you, whether you're going funny, sentimental, or detailed about your journey.
  • You might consider captions tailored to specific circumstances, such as rainbow babies, fertility treatment journeys, multiples, and seasonal or holiday announcements.
  • While some parents these days opt to skip social media announcements altogether and "hard launch" after birth, plenty of caption inspiration exists for those who do want to share the news with friends and family online.

What is a pregnancy announcement caption?

A pregnancy announcement caption is the text attached to a photo or video you share on social media. It could also be a one-liner shown on a letterboard or other sign featured in a social media post. Depending on the theme and your own personality, the caption can be short and snappy, long and detailed, or loaded up with silly pregnancy puns… you name it, the options are endless.
