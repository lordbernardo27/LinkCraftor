About Miriam Carson, B.S.N., R.N.-C

Miriam Carson is a certified registered nurse who earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN) from the University of Pennsylvania. She works as a labor and delivery and outpatient obstetrics nurse at University Medical Centers.

Carson and her husband Michael, an internal medicine physician who specializes in the care of pregnant women with medical issues, live in Long Branch, New Jersey, and have two adult children.

Carson works in the labor and delivery unit and with underserved patients at the outpatient OB/GYN clinic at Riverview Medical Center Red Bank, New Jersey. She is also the assistant manager in a postpartum unit.

Board certifications

Carson is certified through the National Certification Corporation in Inpatient Obstetrics.

Hospital affiliations

Carson is affiliated with Pennsylvania Hospital in Philadelphia; Women & Infants Hospital of Rhode Island; Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital in New Brunswick, New Jersey; and Riverview Medical Center in Red Bank, New Jersey.

Other relevant positions

Carson is a published author in the Journal of Perinatal Education and participates in a nursing help line for new moms.

Awards

Carson was recognized as an Outstanding Nurse Educator by the OB/GYN Residency Program at Rutgers/Robert Wood Johnson University Medical Center in New Brunswick, New Jersey.
