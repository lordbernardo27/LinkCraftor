The 16 Best Baby Stocking Stuffers for Their First Christmas and Beyond

A collage of the best baby stocking stuffers, from left to right: stacking cups, Welly bandages, Bombas baby socks, LesserEvil Lil' Puffs snacks, and an Eric Carle-themed stocking

Quick Summary

The best baby stocking stuffers are age-appropriate and fun for little ones. Useful products like onesies, binkies and developmental toys are a surefire hit.

Growing up, my parents went out of their way to stuff my Christmas stocking with fun surprises. Needless to say, I’ve carried on that tradition in my own household as a mom to four young kids. Over the years, I’ve filled my fair share of the holiday staple with dozens upon dozens of trinkets, toys and treats, and have learned that the best baby stocking stuffers keep the fun going long after each gift has been unwrapped.

Whether you’re celebrating your child’s first Christmas or they’re pushing 2 years old, my recommendations for baby stocking stuffers include proven crowd-pleasers that my own crew loved (think: bubbles, teethers and books), along with recommendations by fellow parents in the What to Expect community.

How I Chose the Best Stocking Stuffers

Stocking stuffers for babies nail that sweet spot of being fun and useful, while still being small enough to squeeze into an oversized sock. To choose the best baby stocking stuffers, I relied on my personal experience as a mom of four. But because every family is different, I also pored over the What to Expect community message boards to see what other parents loved gifting.

Since babies generally fall into the newborn and 2-year-old age range, it’s important to consider where your child lies within that window. For very young children, I’ve found it’s better to choose something entertaining, like a rattle, or to opt for something useful that you as a parent will appreciate, such as binkies and onesies. You can get a bit more creative as your little one — and their development — grows.

I also ensured every item is either tiny to begin with or can be rolled into a smaller size to fit into a stocking. Finally, I kept most of the products to $10 or less. After all, this isn’t the gift category where you want to spend a ton of money.

Baby Stocking Stuffers FAQs

About how much should I anticipate spending on stocking stuffers?

This ultimately depends on your budget, but speaking from experience, you can get a lot of cute stuff for under $10. I included a few items that cost a little more, but only for products that you’ll get a lot of mileage out of. Also, keep this in mind: Your child will have no idea what their stocking stuffers cost. What matters is that they’re a good fit for them.

Where can I buy cute, unique stockings for kids?

You can find adorable stocking options nearly everywhere. However, if you want a few options to start, check out Target, West Elm Kids, Pottery Barn Kids and Maisonette. Amazon is also an excellent spot to score cute, unique stockings at a low price (What to Expect editors recommend Creative Co-Op’s rustic designs.)
