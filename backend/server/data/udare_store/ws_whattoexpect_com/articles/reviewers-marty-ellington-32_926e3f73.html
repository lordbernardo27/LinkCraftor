About Marty Ellington, M.D., M.P.H

Dr. Marty Ellington is a board-certified pediatrician and neonatologist practicing at Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City. He currently serves as Chairman of Pediatrics and Director of Newborn Services at Lenox Hill Hospital. Dr. Ellington received an M.D. as well as an M.P.H. from the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor.

Dr. Ellington completed his residency at UCSF Department of Pediatrics in San Francisco, California, and a fellowship in newborn medicine at Boston Children’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School.

Dr. Ellington has been selected as among the best neonatologists in New York City by the New York Times and New York magazine for numerous years.

Board certifications

Dr. Ellington is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Ellington is affiliated with Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Ellington is Chairman of Pediatrics and Chairman of the Credentials Committees at Lenox Hill Hospital. He is also an assistant professor at the Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell in Hempstead, New York.
