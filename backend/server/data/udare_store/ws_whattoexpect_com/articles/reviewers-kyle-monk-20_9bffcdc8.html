About Kyle Monk, M.D.

Dr. Kyle Monk is a board-certified pediatrician at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles, California. She primarily works in the outpatient setting providing primary care to children and adolescents.

Additionally, she is an attending physician in the newborn nursery at Cedars-Sinai Hospital.

After graduating from Howard University with a degree in biology, Dr. Monk attended Howard University College of Medicine in Washington, D.C. She completed her pediatric residency at New York Presbyterian Hospital, Cornell Campus, in New York City.

Board certifications

Dr. Monk is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Monk is an attending physician at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Monk has a passion for providing care to medically underserved populations and has completed multiple international medical mission trips.

On a local scale, Dr. Monk volunteers by providing free physicals for high school students. Dr. Monk also serves as a mentor to increase minority representation in the medical field with an ultimate goal of addressing disparities in health care.
