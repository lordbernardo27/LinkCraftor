About James Greenberg, M.D.

Dr. James Greenberg is a board-certified general obstetrician/gynecologist and serves as a Vice Chairman of the Department of Obstetrics & Gynecology at Brigham and Women's Hospital in Boston, Massachusetts.

Dr. Greenberg did his residency training at the Brigham and Women's Hospital and Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston, Massachusetts.

He has a Bachelor of Arts degree in history from Amherst College and received his M.D. from New York University School of Medicine.

Board certification

Dr. Greenberg is board-certified by the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Greenberg is affiliated with Brigham and Women's Hospital and Brigham and Women's Faulkner Hospital in Boston.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Greenberg is an associate professor of Obstetrics and Gynecology at Harvard Medical School and a team member of the International Organization for Women and Development (IOWD), which addresses women's health issues in Rwanda.

Publications and research

Dr. Greenberg has published more than 100 articles, abstracts and chapters in a wide variety of scientific media. He is a subject author for UpToDate on C-sections and vaginal deliveries requiring vacuum extraction.

Awards

Dr. Greenberg has been aCastle Connolly Top Doctorsince 2010.
