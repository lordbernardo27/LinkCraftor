Toddler Behavior and Discipline

Toddler behavior can be surprising, frustrating and often out-of-control — but luckily you can solve most behavior problems. And once you find discipline that works (whether it's time-outs or positive reinforcement), you'll spend less time scolding and more time enjoying your tot.
