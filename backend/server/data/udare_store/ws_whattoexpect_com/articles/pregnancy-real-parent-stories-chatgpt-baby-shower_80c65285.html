I Let ChatGPT Plan a Baby Shower — And Let's Just Say, Wow

ChatGPT shower

One mom-to be's bestie explains how AI saved her time, sparked ideas, and added a little magic to the celebration.

By Celia Quillian, as told to Blake Bakkila.

When my best friend spilled the beans that she waspregnant, I instantly knew I wanted to be the one to plan her shower. Fast forward a few months, and here I was with my mom (who also volunteered to help me host), sitting down to organize said shower.

I pulled up ChatGPT, the AI tool that has been my ultimate creative partner for some of my most memorable celebrations: a 4th of July bash, a Bridgerton-inspired bridal shower, and now, I knew I wanted it to help us dream up a jungle-themed baby shower for my best friend.

When we sat down to get started, the theme came easily — the baby’snurserywas already full of sweet jungle details — and with AI’s help, our brainstorming session was so much easier than I ever thought it could be. I didn't know a ton about baby showers, so I was grateful for the inspiration and help.

The results? Four fun games and activities, a snack and dessert spread that was totally on-theme, and a décor piece that doubled as the sweetest nursery keepsake. Here’s what we created — and what we learned along the way.

Let it help you plan your menu

ChatGPT shower food

When it came to food (arguably my favorite part of any party), I wanted ChatGPT’s take on what to serve the mama-to-be and her guests. So I asked: “What are some ideas for light bites, appetizers, snacks, and beverages to serve at a jungle-themed baby shower? Give me 20.”

In seconds, AI delivered a menu that felt like a safari in snack form. Each category came with five suggestions, including a refreshing mocktail option — strawberry lemonade with mint and club soda — that could easily be spiked with vodka or gin for those who wanted it. When I asked ChatGPT to give the drink a jungle-themed name, it came back with “Jungle Juice Punch.” Simple, fun, and totally fitting.

It hit the nail on the head with snacks, too. AI recommended creating platters of “vibrant veggies,” which inspired us to arrange peppers, carrots, and dip into the shape of a lion’s face. Guests couldn’t stop snapping photos — they thought it was adorable. Another hit was a trail mix recipe of banana chips, nuts, and chocolate chips — lovingly dubbed “Monkey Munch Mix.” Cute, right?

Be specific with your ask – you can add more details as you go

Once our menu was set, we moved on to games. I typed in: “What are 20 ideas forbaby shower games?” — but quickly realized I needed to be more specific. Since this was a couple’s shower (and neither mom nor dad wanted to be in the spotlight), I edited my request: “What are 10 good names for a newlywed game-style activity for parents-to-be?” and then, “What are 20 fun questions to ask a mom and dad that they’ll (hopefully) both answer the same?”

ChatGPT suggested thoughtful, heartwarming questions like, “Which family tradition does the other person most want to pass down to the baby?” and “What trait does the other person hope the baby inherits from them?” It even gave us 10 playful game names to choose from.

The result? A game that had the whole room giggling — and the parents-to-be laughing the hardest of all. Comparing their answers side by side was equal parts sweet and hilarious, and it made for one of the most memorable moments of the day.

Think of AI as your visual collaborator

ChatGPT decor

Food and games weren’t the only areas where AI came to the rescue — it also doubled as our décor designer. At the time, Midjourney was the image-generating tool of choice, so I used it to whip up a few sweet illustrations of baby animals. We printed them at Walgreens and displayed them around the party. (Bonus: Those little critters are now framed in the baby’s nursery, so the celebration lives on.)

Now ChatGPT also has image capabilities, so you can simply type in exactly what you want — “a watercolor baby giraffe wearing a party hat,” for example — and get a made-to-order design that fits your vision. Talk about a party planner’s dream sidekick.

Take advantage of how good it is at scouring the web

ChatGPT shower

These days, you don’t even have to spend hours scrolling through shopping sites or juggling multiple tabs. Just tell ChatGPT: “Give me 10 décor items between $10 to $50 I can buy right now,” and it will pull together a curated list in seconds. The same goes for venues — you can plug in your budget, zip code, and must-have details (outdoor space? stroller-friendly? plenty of parking?) and get a tailored shortlist without the endless Googling.

For this party, when asking for its help brainstorming snacks, we went a little more detailed with the prompt after the initial ask because we knew we wanted to keep the costs low, writing: "I'm hosting a jungle-themed baby shower. I'll have approximately 30 guests, and I want to spend no more than $150 on food and drinks for the party. For this party, I'm not intending to serve a full meal but rather a variety of light bites and snacks that include some healthy options and other sweet options. Propose for me a menu for the party that can reasonably fit this budget and my goals, assuring every guest will be able to try everything." ChatGPT gave us those veggie trays and snack mix options that were both affordable — and adorable.

It’s like having a super-organized party planner who does all the legwork, so you can focus on the fun.

Remember that ChatGPT doesn’t have taste — you do!

I like to joke that ChatGPT doesn’t exactly have taste — at least not the way humans do. As much as I wish it could pop out of my laptop, tie on an apron, and handle the cooking and decorating for me, it’s not quite there yet.

What it can do is spark ideas, keep you organized, and take a lot of the mental load off your plate. But the magic touch? That still comes from you. The little details — the ones that reflect the mama-to-be’s personality, her favorite flavors, or the sweet quirks that make her smile — are the ones that matter most. We definitely used what ChatGPT suggested, but we made sure to incorporate some things we knew the mom-to-be loved, like her favorite cookies and pretzels with dip (after all, when the pregnancy cravings hit, we're all about satisfying them!). Happy party planning!
