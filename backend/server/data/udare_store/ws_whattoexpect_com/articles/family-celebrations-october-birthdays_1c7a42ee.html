Get (even more) excited for your autumn arrival with these fun facts.

Due in October? You’re in for a special little one! From their sparkling birthstone to their vibrant birth flower, babies born this month carry a mix of charm, sweetness, and bravery.

Whether they’re destined to be a loving Libra or a stand-by-your-side Scorpio, October babies come into the world during one of the most colorful, cozy times of the year — and there are plenty of fun, festive facts to celebrate about your soon-to-arrive little pumpkin.

Key Takeaways

  • Fall babies get the dreamiest start. October is all crisp air, cozy cuddles, and pumpkin-spiced everything — the perfect backdrop for baby snuggles and seasonal photo ops.
  • Your little one’s birth month is full of charm. With a shimmering opal birthstone and bold marigold flower, October babies shine bright — and are known for their creativity, courage, and heart.
  • There’s magic in every milestone. From festive birthday parties to famous birthday twins, October arrivals are surrounded by inspiration, warmth, and a whole lot of love.
  1. Your October baby will arrive just as cozy season begins. Crisp air, pumpkin everything, and soft blankets — fall babies get the dreamiest backdrop for those first snuggles and newborn photos. “I’m super excited because I love fall and pumpkins etc. I can already picture baby’s 1-year photoshoot,” says What to Expect Community mom cmdjo.
  2. October’s birthstone is the opal — a gem said to bring good luck and positivity . With its rainbow-like shimmer, it’s a fitting match for your October baby’s bright, one-of-a-kind personality.
  3. Your October baby’s birth flower is the marigold, a bloom that symbolizes fierce love and creativity. Like this bold, golden flower, October babies often have a warmth and vibrancy that make them hard to miss.
  4. October babies fall under the signs of Libra (Sep 23 - Oct 22) or Scorpio (Oct 23- Nov 21). Libras are charming, fair, and full of heart, while Scorpios are loyal, brave, and deeply passionate — both destined to make a big impact.
  5. October babies tend to be optimistic and independent . Studies have found that people born in autumn often have a sunny outlook and a strong sense of self — qualities that might shine through in your little one.
  6. You’ll have fun planning birthdays. From pumpkin patch parties to Halloween-themed bashes, October birthdays come with built-in inspiration for adorable (and easy!) celebrations. “October always has so many fun things going on,” says What to Expect Community mom mimipink6.
  7. Speaking of birthdays, your little one could share a birthday with some pretty inspiring names. Think Neil deGrasse Tyson (October 5), Eleanor Roosevelt (October 11), Drake (October 24), or Ciara (October 25) — not bad company to be born into!
  8. October birthdays come with plenty of reasons to celebrate. Your baby might arrive on World Smile Day (the first Friday in October), Columbus Day (the second Monday of the month), or even make a Halloween debut onOctober 31.
  9. October babies are born under the glow of change. As the leaves turn and the season shifts, your little one arrives during one of the most beautiful times of year — a perfect symbol of new beginnings. “I think the fall coziness will be so nice when we get to snuggle the newborn baby all day, and I can’t wait to have them here for the holidays,” says What to Expect Community mom Apg27.
