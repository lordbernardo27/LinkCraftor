About Jennifer Butt, M.D., F.A.C.O.G.

Dr. Jennifer Butt is a board-certified OB/GYN practicing in New York City.

Dr. Butt was part of the B.A./M.D. program at Rutgers University in New Brunswick, New Jersey— receiving her B.A. in Biological Studies and M.D. from the Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School. She completed her residency in obstetrics and gynecology at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital.

She currently sees patients at her own private practice, Upper East Side OB/GYN, located in Manhattan.

Board certification

Dr. Butt is board-certified with the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology, and is a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Butt is affiliated with Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City.

Other relevant positions

Inspired to start her own business and deliver top-quality personalized care to her patients, Dr. Butt founded the practiceUpper East Side OB/GYNin 2018.

She lives with her husband and son in downtown Manhattan.
