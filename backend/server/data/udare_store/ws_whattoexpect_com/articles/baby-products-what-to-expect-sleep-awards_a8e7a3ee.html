What to Expect’s Sleep Award Winners Include Everything From Swaddles to Sound Machines

What to Expect Sleep Awards 2025

Whether you’re constantly waking up for late-night feeds, silently begging your newborn to fall back asleep or struggling through a toddler sleep regression, quality Zzzs are probably on your wishlist. And while there are countless products out there that promise to help you and your little one get more shut-eye, not everything lives up to the hype. Luckily, our community of moms have “been there, done that.” And they recently cast their votes in the sixth annual What to Expect Sleep Awards to tell you which are actually worth your hard-earned dollar.

How We Chose What to Expect’s Sleep Award Winners

Throughout the month of April, more than 800 parents cast their votes for their baby bedtime heroes across 18 different categories; everything from swaddles and sound machines to lotions and gliders. The result? An A-list of the best sleep-related baby products on the market.

Winners selected based on which nominee received the most votes. We also combed through thousands of write-in comments to learn exactly why parents love the following products.

Best Swaddle

SwaddleMe Original Swaddle

Material: Cotton

Why We Love It

Many caregivers swaddle their baby at night in hopes of preventing flailing arms from interrupting precious sleep. But swaddling can be tricky — especially when you're exhausted — which is why many parents turn to specially designedswaddle blanketswith time-saving features like zippers or snaps.

According to the What to Expect community, the best of the bunch is theSwaddleMe Original Swaddle— an affordable, user-friendly option that’s also a back-to-back winner in the What to Expect Sleep Awards.

Using it is simple, open the swaddle’s “wings,” place your baby on top with their legs inside the bottom pouch, wrap one wing snugly around your baby, followed by the other, and fasten using the large Velcro tabs. You can adjust the wings for a secure, custom fit — ideal for even the wiggliest sleepers. The bottom zipper makes those late-night diaper changes much easier.

Another bonus: the roomy leg pocket gives your baby plenty of space to kick, which parents love. Many also note how well the swaddle holds up, even after frequent washes — a huge plus given the inevitability of overnight blowouts and spit-up.

Just remember: stop using any swaddle as soon as your baby begins to roll over.
