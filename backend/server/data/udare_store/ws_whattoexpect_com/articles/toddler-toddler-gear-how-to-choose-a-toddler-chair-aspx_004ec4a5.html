How to Choose a Toddler Chair

toddler chair

When your child is ready for a throne of her own, here's how to find a seat that'll suit her.

As you’ve no doubt learned from your little shadow, tiny tykes love to imitate the grown-ups in their lives. Once they hit toddlerhood, kids want to eat, clean, and yes, even relax just like their parents do, so a toddler chair is a surefire hit (especially if your wee one lovesplaying house). From basic wooden and plastic seats to mini recliners and plush club chairs with matching ottomans, it’s easy to find a chill-out spot for your tot that’s practically identical to your own chair of choice. And if your little one is sitting pretty in her pint-sized perch, she’ll be more likely to spend time there, happily entertaining herself. These tips will put you in the driver’s seat as you search out the perfect toddler chair:

  • The right size. Not every toddler chair is the perfect height for your small fry, so it’s best to bring your little lounger to the store to give a couple of chairs a try before you invest in this piece oftoddler gear. Her feet should touch the floor when she’s seated, though it’s not necessary that they rest flat against it. The goal is for your toddler to be able to sit down and get out of the chair on her own, and if her feet hit the ground when she’s sitting, she should have no trouble making those moves all by herself.
  • A sturdy make. Safety shouldn’t take a backseat to style. Forchildren’s safety, look at the manufacturer’s instructions for the suggested maximum height, weight and age of the child using the chair. You can also look for a JPMA Seal (Juvenile Products Manufacturers Association) which means the product has been tested on behalf of the manufacturer to meet federal and state safety standards. Why? Well, your toddler’s not going to pack on that many pounds before preschool, but she’ll probably run into her chair, try knocking it over, and even stand on it. A sturdy chair means it can withstand that kind of rough play.
  • The right age range. Most chairs for toddlers are designed for children between the ages of 18 months and four years old, but it’s still worth checking the age recommendations on the packaging before you buy one, in case the chair is intended for bigger kids. Always look for the manufacturer’s suggested weight capacity and top age for the chair.
  • The best material for the intended use. Basic plastic or wood chairs are great if your tot and her playmates will be using them at tiny tables for children, while an upholstered chair works well next to your critter’s book collection. Crazy about coordinating? You’ll be able to find a toddler chair to match practically every material on the grown-up versions scattered around your house.
