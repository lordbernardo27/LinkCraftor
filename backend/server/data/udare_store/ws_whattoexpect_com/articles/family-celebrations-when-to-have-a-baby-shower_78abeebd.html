When to have a baby shower

Although there's no right time to celebrate your newest arrival, there are a few things you may want to consider when deciding when to have a baby shower.

Not sure when you should have yourbaby shower? The short answer is that there's no "right" time — some parents opt for a shower early on in their pregnancies, while others wait until a week or two before theirdue dates.

Ultimately, the timing of your baby shower depends on what works for you, your family and the person hosting the event.

When should you have a baby shower?

Still, many moms-to-be report a sweet spot of the early- to mid-third trimester, when they are far enough along to start showing and have finished setting up their baby registry, but not so close to their due date that they're feeling overly tired or rundown. (Although some pregnant women have energy up until the end, others may not feel enthusiastic about socializing late in the third trimester.)

How much time you have after the shower is another consideration: Cut it too close, and you might not be able to finish writingbaby shower thank-you notesbefore your little one arrives. Many parents also appreciate a few weeks to unpack their gifts, wash new clothes and set everything up in baby'snursery.

Because there's always a chance your little one willarrive early, having a buffer of a few weeks between your shower and due date may also give you some peace of mind.

Of course, everyone's situation is different. For some parents-to-be, their ideal baby shower timing might conflict with big events when guests will be out of town. (It's sometimes tricky to schedule afall baby showeror awinter baby showeraround the holidays!)

And if you've hired an outside venue to host your shower, you'll may have a more limited selection of dates. You'll likely also want to ensure that close family and friends can make the date you ultimately choose. (On the other hand, if you're opting for avirtual baby shower, scheduling tends to be a lot easier.)

Below, What to Expect community members share how far along they were when they had their baby showers — more proof that there's no right time (or way) to celebrate a new arrival.
