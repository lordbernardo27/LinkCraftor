Your baby’s birth chart, decoded

Think of it like a cosmic baby book: a one-of-a-kind map of where the Sun, Moon, and planets were when your little one took her first breath. Each detail — from her rising sign to where Venus was hanging out — offers sweet insight into your baby’s personality, emotions, and growing style.

What you’ll see

Your child’s chart will look like a big starry wheel, divided into 12 “houses” (kind of like celestial rooms). Each house hosts a zodiac sign — maybe Leo in the first, Pisces in the twelfth — and the planets take their places throughout, forming a beautiful sky story.

For example: If the Moon was shining through the sign of Taurus in the second house at the time of birth, that gives your little one a Taurus Moon in the 2nd house, which could hint at a love for cozy routines, sweet treats, and lots of snuggles.

Astrologers look at all these pieces — signs, houses, planets, and their little cosmic conversations (called “aspects”) — to paint a fuller picture of your baby’s emotional rhythm, natural gifts, and soulful sparkle.

A few helpful notes before you begin

You don’t need to share your child’s full name unless you want to. Providing a nickname or initial (or even leaving it blank) is just fine. The chart is all about your baby's birth details.

For the location field, just pop in the city where your little one was born. (Typing “Boston” should give you dropdown options — go with the one that fits best.)

The exact time of birth gives the clearest chart. If you don’t know it, choose “I don’t know” — the chart will still be meaningful, just without a rising sign or house placements.

Fun fact: The Moon changes signs about every 2.5 days, so if your lovebug was born on a cusp (when one sign’s ending and another’s beginning), that timing can shift things a bit. If you’re not sure, we’ll help guide you through it.

Whether you’re a seasoned astrology lover or just feeling starry-eyed, your child’s birth chart is a tender, timeless way to explore what makes your baby so uniquely her. It’s less about prediction and more about connection.

FAQs About Birth Chart Calculator

How to read your baby’s birth chart

Astrological birth charts can be tricky to read, but our birth chart provides a full description of your baby’s astrological traits, even if you don’t have much experience with astrology or birth charts. Just plug in your baby’s data points and let our tool do the hard work!

Can I use the birth chart calculator before my baby is born?

Yes, you can test out the birth chart calculator with your due date and anticipated location and time of birth. But until your baby is born, you won’t be able to know their unique chart. That’s something to look forward after you meet your little one!

How accurate is an astrological birth chart?

Your baby could have a lot of the personality traits described in their birth chart…or not! Astrological birth charts are a lot of fun, but your baby will be a unique person with a personality all their own.
