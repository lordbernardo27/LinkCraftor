Quick Summary

Having watched my sons grow from music-making toddlers to budding stage performers, I believe the best musical toys for toddlers are designed for open-ended play, prioritize safety, are made from durable materials and encourage a life-long love of music.

When my kids were toddlers, their favorite toys were the musical ones: mini drum sets, ukuleles, and light-up xylophones dominated the playroom. The best musical toys for toddlers keep them entertained and help them expend energy, but in my experience, they also help build a foundational love of music. Now in their tween years, my children sing in a local choir and perform in musicals.

According to child development experts, playing with musical toys can encourage early cognitive development, helping toddlers develop coordination and laying the groundwork for future STEM skills. From simple shakers to toddler-friendly guitars and keyboards, there’s a wide variety of musical toys that can make your home come alive with sound (and sometimes sweet chaos!).

How I Chose the Best Musical Toys For Toddlers

Drawing from personal experience, I know anything that lets the kids shake, drum or dance is an instant hit. When choosing the best musical toys for toddlers, I kept safety top of mind, prioritizing options that are age-appropriate and designed to stand up to lots of play. To refine the list, I researched over 40 products and leaned on recommendations from the What to Expect community. I also sought insight fromClaire Goss, Senior Manager of Family and Client Support atBright Horizons, andDr. Dana Suskind, a pediatrician and co-director of the TMW Center for Early Learning + Public Health.

Based on Dr. Suskind’s advice andAmerican Academy of Pediatrics(AAP) recommendations, I sought toys with volume control or child-appropriate decibel levels. I also I looked for open-ended musical toys that let children experiment with different sounds and rhythms while ensuring they were made from safe materials. Finally, I chose toys that are easy for little hands to operate, so toddlers can enjoy them independently.

What to Consider When Buying a Musical Toy

Age-appropriateness: In addition to looking at the recommended age range, evaluate the toy as a whole. Look for easy-to-use features like large buttons or knobs that your child can use independently. For younger toddlers, consider more simple toys like shakers, tambourines or play mats that encourage movement and sensory exploration, while older toddlers may want something more advanced like a keyboard, microphone, or drum set to hone in on fine motor skills and creativity.
