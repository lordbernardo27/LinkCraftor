baby girl names

With so many baby girl name options out there, choosing the right one can be a difficult decision. Here are the 1,000 top girl names in 2025 to help inspire you.

What's in a baby girl name? It can be challenging to choose the perfect name for your daughter but luckily, there's plenty of inspiration out there to help guide this important decision, whether you go for a classic, sweet girl name or a cool and unique moniker.

A great place to start your baby name search is theSocial Security Administration (SSA), which releases a list of the 1,000 most popular baby names for girls and boys every year. The list below references SSA data from 2025.

Can't decide on the perfect name for your new bundle of joy? Run your ideas by other moms on theWhat to Expectapp.

Here, browse the top 1,000 baby girl names for inspiration (or to see if your little one's name made the list!).

Key Takeaways

  • The Social Security Administration releases a list of the top 1,000 baby girl names in the U.S. every year, and the 2025 data is packed with both timeless classics and fresh, trendy picks.
  • Names like Olivia, Charlotte, and Emma continue to hold strong at the top, while unique choices like Klarity, Rynlee, and Ailanny are rising in popularity.
  • With so many beautiful options to explore, this list is a great starting point whether you're naming your baby girl, looking for inspiration, or just curious about name trends.
