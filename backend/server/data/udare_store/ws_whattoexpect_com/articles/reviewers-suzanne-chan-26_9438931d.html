About Suzanne Chan, D.N.P., C.L.C., C.P.S.T.

Suzanne Chan is a board-certified pediatric nurse practitioner working in the Infant Cardiac NICU at NewYork-Presbyterian Morgan Stanley Children's Hospital in New York City. In addition to her expertise in labor and delivery, Chan is also a certified lactation counselor (CLC) and a Certified Passenger Safety Technician (CPST).

Chan has not only earned a Bachelor of Science and Master of Science in Nursing, but also a Doctorate of Nursing Practice from Columbia University in New York City.

Board certification

Chan is board certified by the Pediatric Nursing Certification Board.

Hospital affiliation

Chan is affiliated with NewYork-Presbyterian Morgan Stanley Children's Hospital in New York City.

Other relevant positions

Chan is a member of the American Nurses Association (ANA), the National Association of Pediatric Nurse Practitioners (NAPNAP), the American Association of Nurse Practitioners (AANP), and the American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP).

She lives in New York with her husband, three children, and their beloved dog.
