About Lauren Crosby, M.D., F.A.A.P.

Dr. Lauren Crosby is a pediatrician in Beverly Hills, California, and a nationally-recognized parenting expert.

After graduating from Smith College and the UCLA School of Medicine, Dr. Crosby trained at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center, where she served as Chief Resident.

She is currently in private practice at La Peer Pediatrics in Beverly Hills.

Board certifications

Dr. Crosby is board-certified by the American Board of Pediatrics and is a Fellow of the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Crosby is an attending physician at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Crosby is a member of the American Academy of Pediatrics Council on Communications in Media and an official spokeswoman for the American Academy of Pediatrics.

She is a pediatric consultant and medical correspondent for television news, is on the Advisory Board of the Make-A-Wish Foundation, and is a founding member of Hope in A Suitcase.

Dr. Crosby is also the mother of two sons.
