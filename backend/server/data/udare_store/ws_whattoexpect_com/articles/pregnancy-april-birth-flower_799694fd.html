What are the April birth flowers?

Sweet Pea: sweet as a pea, beautiful as a butterfly

Sweet peas are best known for their enchanting scent: a delightful mix of oranges, honey and jasmine — but don't disregard their beauty — climbing up a wall or trellis, sweet peas' colorful, silky April blooms could be mistaken for butterflies mid-flight. Originally discovered in southern Italy in the 1600's, they have been shared around the world by botanists and plant lovers, these easy-to-grow, aromatic annuals are welcome in any garden! Incorporating the daisy and sweet pea into your family's life

Integrating your little one's birth flowers into your home or every day happenings can bring joy to all, but these flowers also serve as a reminder of your baby’s birth season and month as well as their personality and their spirit. Whether you want to head outside and add some color to your yard, or you want to lighten up your child's room, here are some creative ideas:

  • Watch them grow. Both daisies and sweet peas are easy to grow outdoors, though sweet peas prefer cooler weather. Make sure to give your sweet peas a trellis or fence to climb!
  • Embrace a neutral pallet. Are you in love with the neutral nursery trend? As the parent of an April baby, you're in luck! With daisy's whites and creams, these flowers make the perfect gently-hued nursery decor. Check out this DIYdaisy mobileor just search "rattan daisy mirror" and all your design dreams are sure to come true.
  • Go retro. Prefer something more polychromatic and playful? Take some inspiration from the 80's and 90's "flower power" decor themes, which stars the daisy flower and lots of pastel colors. Try your hand at a pastel-colored nursery or bedroom, and add crocheted or chenille daisy pillows and daisy decals for your walls.
  • Get crafty. This DIYyarn flower wall of daisiesis bound to make a statement and your young one will learn so much from testing out all of the different textures. While inexpensive, this project can be a little time consuming, so save it for a rainy day at home.
  • Don't forget the sweet peas! While certainly less popular than daisy decor, with sweet peas, the road less traveled is definitively sweet. Framed prints or photos of sweet peas make beautiful wall decor. Not to mention, a spritz here and there of sweet-pea infused oil or perfume around your home (when safe for your child) is a great way to enjoy the enchanting scent of this special flower year round.
