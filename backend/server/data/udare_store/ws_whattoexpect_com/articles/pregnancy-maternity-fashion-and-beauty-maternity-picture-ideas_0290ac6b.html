Maternity photo shoot

Planning a maternity photo session? Here's inspiration for how to make the shoot uniquely yours.

Taking photos during your pregnancy helps capture your body's week-by-week transformation along with your feelings of anticipation as you wait for your family to grow.

When it comes tomaternity photos, you've got tons of options. Different poses, props, backdrops, and even pets can reflect the personality and style of the parents-to-be.

"I always recommend that my clients choose a location that is meaningful for them and helps to tell their story," saysSara Wight, a New York City- and Washington D.C.-based photographer.

That could be the site of a meaningful moment, a beautiful vista, or simply the comforts of your living room. Think of spots that bring you joy, nourishment, and inspiration for your pregnancy photo shoot, she recommends.

No matter how you pose — or who joins you in the photo — keep the focus on the bump, Wight advises. "That might seem obvious, but sometimes it's unnoticeable at first glance if you are looking at [the mom-to-be] straight on," she says. Wight's goal when taking maternity pictures: a photo where the bump is showcased in a natural, flattering way.

Not sure where to begin? Browse this list for a little maternity photo shoot inspiration.

Key Takeaways

  • Maternity photos are a fun way to capture your growing bump and all the excitement that comes with it. From simple poses to playful props, there's no shortage of ways to make the moment feel uniquely yours.
  • Meaningful locations and natural, flattering angles help keep the focus right where it belongs — on your bump. Think of locations and poses that spark joy and show off your natural glow.
  • Whether you keep it sweet, silly, or full-on festive, there's a maternity photo idea for every parent-to-be. Think about what would be the most meaningful way for you to tell your family's story.

Maternity photo poses

A growing bump is the natural star of any maternity photo. But figuring out the best way to pose isn't always easy. Let these beautiful photos inspire you on how to approach your own photo session.

Looking down at your bump

maternity photo pose

No need to reinvent the wheel! This mom-to-be proves that a loving look at your growing baby bump plus a natural backdrop is a no-fail combination.

Cross-legged

maternity photo at home

A casual photo, with legs crossed at home, captures this mom at ease in herthird trimester. Just don't wait to go too close to your due date. "I'm a maternity and newborn photographer, and I encourage my clients to have their maternity session between 28 and 34 weeks," says What to Expect Community mom elliesmom24. "It's the perfect sweet spot. Anything later and you might feel way too swollen and blah, or you might entirely miss the session because baby came earlier. It's happened before!"
