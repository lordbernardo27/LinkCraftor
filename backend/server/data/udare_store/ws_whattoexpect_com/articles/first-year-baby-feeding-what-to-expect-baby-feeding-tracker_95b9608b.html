It's also a lot to remember, especially when you're running on (very) little sleep and balancing everything else that comes with new parenthood. Fortunately, ababy trackerlike the one in the What to Expect app can help.

What is a baby tracker?

A baby tracker does exactly what you'd expect — it helps you document all the details of your little one's eating habits, diaper changes, sleep,tummy timesessions, and even your pumping sessions if you have them, plus other details of your little one's daily routine.

A typical baby tracker includes:

  • A way to track breastfeeding sessions, bottle-feedings and pumping breaks, including a timer to record how long they take.
  • Feeding and sleep history over time, so you can look back at patterns and see how they change as your baby gets older.
  • A way to track diaper changes, including when each one happens, what's in the diaper, and the total number of diapers changed in a day.
  • A place to record details related to your baby's feedings, diaper changes, sleep patterns, tummy time, and any other facets of her daily routine you want to keep track of.

Benefits of using a baby tracker

There are a lot of upsides to using a baby tracker. It can help you answer common questions like:

  • What time did my baby's most recent feed start?
  • How many times has she eaten today?
  • What breast should I offer next?
  • When did my baby last nap?
  • How many ounces was that last bottle?
  • How much milk did I pump yesterday?
  • How long has it been since the last change?
  • How many diapers have we gone through today?
  • How many minutes of tummy time did my baby do?
  • When does my baby typically go to bed?

Recording your baby's feedings, diapers, sleep, tummy time, and other details in a tracker like this means you also have all the important info in one place. That way, you can identify patterns in how your little one is growing and developing, and adjust if necessary with your pediatrician's guidance.

Panicked thoughts like "Wait, how long has it been since she last ate?!" can quickly go away thanks to this tool.
