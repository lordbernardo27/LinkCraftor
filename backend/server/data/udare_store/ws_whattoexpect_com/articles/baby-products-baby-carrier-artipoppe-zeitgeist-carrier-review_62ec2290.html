Is the Artipoppe Zeitgeist Baby Carrier Worth the Steep Price Tag? One Mom's Candid Opinion

Mom using Artipoppe baby carrier

A mom of two shares her honest thoughts after testing out the pricey baby carrier.

When I was pregnant for the first time, I added the iconicArtipoppe Zeitgeist baby carrierto my registry on a whim: It was expensive, yes, but stunning; the most stylishbaby carrierI had ever seen and precisely what my babywearing dreams were made of. Four years and two children later, I’m still in love withthe Artipoppe Zeitgeist’s style— but it’s the carrier’s cozy comfort and unexpected durability that has made it a beloved, trusted accessory throughout my motherhood journey (of which babywearing has been a hugely important part).

If you’re not yet acquainted,Artipoppe is a luxury babywearing brand that’s known for its great style and quality — and for being a favorite among fashion-forward celebrities like Hailey Beiber, Hilary Duff, Kylie Jenner, Kelly Rowland, and more. (Seriously, a lot more. Take a look.)

Artipoppe makes three primary products: a baby wrap, a ring sling, and theZeitgeist baby carrier— its most popular product, and the one I own. I used this carrier with my daughter (now 4) from birth through toddlerhood. Today, I’m about 16 months into using it with my son (and we’re still going strong!). In this review, I’ll share my honest opinion on the Zeitgeist’s features and answer the one question that everyone always asks: Is the Zeitgeist worth the price tag?

What is the Artipoppe Zeitgeist baby carrier?

The Zeitgeist baby carrier is an adjustable, buckled carrier with cross-back straps that can be worn in three ergonomic positions: front carry facing in, front carry facing out, and back carry. It’s incredibly comfortable to wear and feels more like a soft baby wrap than, say, a bulky piece of hiking gear. It’s versatile and long-lasting (it’ll hold a child from the newborn stage to the toddler years), and it’s made of sustainably sourced materials.

To my knowledge, there’s no baby carrier brand that offers a higher quality carrier in such an extensive, beautiful range of styles. The cost of the Zeitgeist reflects this — it’s one of the most expensive options on the market. While nobody needs a carrier that costs upwards of $370 (more on pricing below), it’s a lovely option for parents who want to invest in something that’s going to look stunning, complement their unique sense of style, and meet the highest standards for sustainability and luxury materials. (And this won’t be everyone! If you’re seeking similar vibes at a lower price point, skip down to the bottom of this post for suggestions on the best Artipoppe alternatives.)

The base-level price of the Zeitgeist starts at $370 for certain prints in fabrics, like linen and cotton. (These are a great option; I own theLeopard Classic in cotton, which is what my personal comments in this review are based on, and I wholeheartedly recommend this fabric.) The next level — consisting of specialty prints or fabrics like organic hemp, organic cashmere, organic velvet or denim — ranges between $390 and $790.

And then for the true luxury seeker, there are even some designs made with a $3,750 blend of vicuña and cashmere that’s available in several prints. (Vicuña wool is known for being one of the finest and most expensive fabrics in the world; the animals can only be shorn once every three years, and after each shearing they are tagged and released back into the wild in certain areas of South America.)

Key features of the Artipoppe Zeitgeist baby carrier:

  • Weight capacity: 7 to 45 pounds
  • Carry positions: Three ergonomic options; front carry facing in, front carry facing out, and back carry
  • Comfort features for caregiver: Cross-back shoulder straps for even weight distribution; padded shoulder straps for optimal neck and shoulder support
  • Comfort features for baby: Padded leg openings; facing-out insert; adjustable neck cushion
  • Safety: Acknowledged “hip-healthy” by the International Hip Dysplasia Institute; complies with the American ASTM F2236 and European CEN/TR 16512-2015 safety standards for baby carriers
  • Care instructions: Varies by material and fabric; the cotton version reviewed here is machine washable

My honest experience — the pros

If I had to succinctly describe my experience with the Artipoppe Zeitgeist, I’d tell you that this carrier is like the perfect pair of jeans: so comfortable and cute that you’ll find yourself wearing it daily, when running errands or hanging around the house — but you can also dress it up for a special occasion. It’s comfortable, it works with every outfit, and it feels easy and effortless. But let’s dive into the details a bit more…

Mom wearing baby in Artipoppe baby carrier

The Artipoppe Zeitgeist offers the best of both babywearing worlds.

The Zeitgeist combines the best of both babywearing worlds: It has the light, cozy, natural feel of a baby wrap (those intimate, cocoon-like vibes are so nice — especially for newborns and naps!). But it also boasts the support, stability and convenience of something with buckles (it can clip on or off in seconds with zero hassle, and it can be worn all day without needing to readjust).

It grows with your baby—from newborn to toddler.

Thanks to a few thoughtful details and four sizing positions, the Zeitgeist is comfortable for babies of all ages — from snoozy newborns to squirmy toddlers (or, more specifically, from 7 pounds to 45 pounds). One of my favorite features is the adjustable neck cushion, which can be put up when your baby needs more support around the head (great for smaller babies or napping), and then quickly strapped down when the baby wants a better view or more range of motion.

The Artipoppe Zeitgeist is shockingly durable and easy to clean.

Confession: I’ve had my Zeitgeist for more than four years, and it frequently gets thrown on my muddy, sandy, sticky car floor. Still, I’ve only needed to spot clean it a few times. Yes, I’ve been lucky to not have any major spills or diaper disasters happen while wearing it; but the fabric is so durable and breathable that dust, dirt, and spills seem to wipe right off. (It also probably helps thatmy carrieris a deep chocolate color.) My babies have played with it and pulled on it for years, and not a single stitch has unraveled; it’s in the exact same condition as it was when I unboxed it. And in the case that the carrier were to need a major clean, I love knowing it’s safe to toss into the washing machine.

This carrier feels like an extension of your outfit — not a piece of baby gear.

Many carriers are defined by a bulky silhouette, utilitarian straps and buckles, and a big, brand-name tag stuck front and center — but you’ll find none of that with an Artipoppe carrier. The Zeitgeist is actually a beautiful complement to any outfit: Thesimpler styleswill blend seamlessly with whatever you’re wearing, while the bolder designs (like thisswan printorthis fringed edition) function as a fashion focal point — kind of like wearing a coat or bag that ‘makes’ an outfit.

Mom wearing Artipoppe baby carrier with stylish outfit

The Artipoppe Zeitgeist is breastfeeding-friendly.

For moms who wish to breastfeed while baby-wearing, the Artipoppe Zeitgeist is a great option: It provides enough roominess and flexibility, and the straps can be loosened for additional space if necessary.

…And the cons of the Artipoppe Zeitgeist

The price tag is high.

The Artipoppe Zeitgeist is one of the most expensive carriers on the market, ranging from $370 to $3,750, which unfortunately makes it inaccessible for many.

Some fabrics may be trickier to clean.

While my cotton carrier can be thrown in the washing machine, several of the other fabrics (like cashmere and vicuña) can only be washed by hand.

There’s no chest strap to tighten when wearing in back-carry position.

While I personally haven’t found the lack of a chest strap to be a problem in back-carry position, I’ve heard from some moms who say they really need the extra support of a chest strap when back-carrying very squirmy or active toddlers.

The single size may not fit all bodies.

The carrier is available in one size, and some online reviews have pointed out that the Zeitgeist might not be a fit for larger body types.

Tips and tricks for using the Artipoppe

I could tell you all about how easy it is to put on the Zeitgeist — but Artipoppe madea series of quick videos to show you the different carry options, and they’re extremely helpful.

A few more things I want to point out…

  • Once you adjust and tighten your straps, you can roll them up and tie them off with small, elastic loops that are positioned on the straps. This means you won’t have any strap excess hanging down.
  • To wear the baby in a forward-facing position, be sure to attach the facing-out insert to the inside of the carrier (included standard with all carriers, and available to purchase separately in the case of loss). This insert narrows the body panel so the baby’s arms can wrap around it, and it provides extra support to the baby’s chest — ensuring a comfortable, ergonomic position.
  • The carrier comes equipped with black safety buttons and elastic safety loops — you’ll see how to use those in thedemonstration videos.
  • For cooler climes and winter months, there’s a cozywinter insertthat offers additional warmth (sold separately from the carrier).

So…is it worth it?

Everyone always wants to know if the Artipoppe is “worth it” — and while my personal answer would be an emphatic absolutely, it depends on your own personal priorities.

Do you plan on babywearing often? When you see someone wearing the Artipoppe, does your head turn or do you stop scrolling? Do you care if your carrier looks like an elevated, intentional accessory vs. a piece of babywearing gear? Is the sourcing and sustainability of your carrier important to you?

If you answered “yes” to any or all of these questions, it’s likely that you’ll love the Artipoppe Zeitgeist. (I certainly do.)

When I look back on the past four (going on five!) years of motherhood, the Zeitgeist played a starring role in so many happy, beautiful memories: sunset walks on the beach, strolls around the zoo, and cuddles literally everywhere we went. (Can I just say how wonderful it feels to be able to spontaneously kiss the top of your baby’s head while you’re out grocery shopping?)

The Zeitgeist helped me get through the more difficult moments, too. As a first-time mom, I vividly remember strapping my newborn to my chest and pacing around our house for hours in the early evening — it was the only way I could make it through the“witching hour,” when close contact was the only thing that soothed those nightly bouts of fussiness. And when my second baby’s frequent cries and sensitivity turned tocolic, I quickly realized that the only way to keep him happy and comfortable was to strap him in the Zeitgeist and keep him close to my heart for hours on end.

I could have done these things in any carrier, of course (as long as it was safe and comfortable). But the Zeitgeist wasn’t just a piece of baby gear; it was a beautiful, stylish carrier that felt like a joy to wear. It felt very much like me at a time when maintaining my sense of selfhood was particularly important.

My advice? If style and aesthetics are important to you, and you plan to babywear often, find a carrier that you’ll fall in love with. If the Artipoppe Zeitgeist feels like a big splurge, consider asking friends and family to contribute to a group fund — or just take a few other extraneous items off your newborn shopping list (there are so many things we really don’t need, while a carrier is something you could use for hours every day). Prioritizing what’s important to you is a hugely important lesson when it comes to so many things in motherhood—starting with your baby gear.

Dupes to consider

Is the Artipoppe carrier amazing? Yes. Are there also other, equally amazing carriers out there — with different features, at lower price points? Also yes! The ideal carrier for one parent will be very different than the ideal carrier for another — whether that’s because of features, budget, or both. That’s why I’ve curated a range of top-notch alternatives if you’re looking for a different carrier with similar vibes.

  • Wildbird Aerial Carrier($268): Based on appearance alone, the Wildbird Aerial Carrier is the closest style I’ve seen to the Artipoppe Zeitgeist. Love that there are no big, branded tags on this one.
  • ErgoBaby Omni Dream($199): This carrier is one of many options from trusted brand ErgoBaby and has many similarities to the Zeitgeist: a minimal design, extreme softness (it’s cotton, which is key for comfort and breathability), plenty of versatility and carry positions, and a range of stylish patterns.
  • Beco Gemini($80): A simple, cross-back design and reasonable price point make this carrier a solid option for those who want Artipoppe vibes on a budget. Made with 100% cotton and available in a wide range of colors and prints, it’s soft and breathable — and there’s a style for everyone.
