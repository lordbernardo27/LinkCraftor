About Allison Bloom, D.O., M.P.H.

Dr. Allison Bloom is a board-certified OB/GYN and board-eligible reproductive endocrinology and infertility specialist practicing at Main Line Fertility in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Dr. Bloom graduated from the University of Massachusetts with a Bachelor of Science before earning her Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine (D.O.) and Masters in Public Health (M.P.H.) from Nova Southeastern University College of Osteopathic Medicine in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

She then completed her residency in Obstetrics and Gynecology at Drexel University College of Medicine/Hahnemann University Hospital in Philadelphia as well as a fellowship in Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility with Main Line Fertility/Drexel University College of Medicine.

Board certifications

Dr. Bloom is board-certified by the American College of Obstetrics and Gynecology.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Bloom is affiliated with Lankenau Medical Center, Bryn Mawr Hospital, Paoli Hospital and Chester County Hospital.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Bloom is a member of the clinical faculty at Lankenau Hospital and a board member of the Philadelphia Area Reproductive Endocrine Society.

Dr. Bloom is also affiliated with the American Society for Reproductive Medicine (ASRM), Gay and Lesbian Medical Association, American Osteopathic Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology (AOBOG), American Osteopathic Association (AOA) and American Medical Association (AMA).

Awards

Dr. Bloom has received a number of awards, including:

  • Educational Chief Resident, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Drexel University College of Medicine
  • Medical Student Teaching Award Recipient, Drexel University College of Medicine, two-time recipient
  • Resident CREOG Exam Achievement Award, four-time recipient
  • Phi Sigma Alpha, National Osteopathic Scholastic Society inductee
  • Sigma Sigma Phi, National Honorary Service Fraternity, President
  • Matthew Terry Scholarship recipient for scholastic excellence, service, empathy and congeniality
