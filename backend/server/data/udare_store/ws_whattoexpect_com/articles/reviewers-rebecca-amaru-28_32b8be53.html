About Rebecca Amaru, M.D.

Dr. Rebecca Amaru is a board-certified OB/GYN practicing in New York City.

Dr. Amaru completed her OB/GYN residency at Mount Sinai Hospital and received her M.D. from the Mount Sinai School of Medicine, both in New York City.

She is currently in private practice at Brasner, Blumberg & Amaru in Manhattan.

Board certification

Dr. Amaru is board-certified with the American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology, and is a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists.

Hospital affiliations

Dr. Amaru is affiliated with Mount Sinai Hospital in New York City.

Other relevant positions

Dr. Amaru is an assistant clinical professor in the OB/GYN Department at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai. Each year, she serves as preceptor for a group of third-year medical students during their clerkship in OB/GYN, and teaches the first-year medical students how to take a gynecologic history in their "Art and Science of Medicine" course.

Her practice interests include low- and moderate-risk obstetrics, breastfeeding support, adolescent gynecology, sexual and psychosocial health throughout womens' lifespans.

Dr. Amaru lives with her husband and four daughters in White Plains, New York.

Awards

Dr. Amaru has been aCastle Connolly Top Doctorsince 2017 and was an Exceptional Women in Medicine awardee in 2019 and 2020.
