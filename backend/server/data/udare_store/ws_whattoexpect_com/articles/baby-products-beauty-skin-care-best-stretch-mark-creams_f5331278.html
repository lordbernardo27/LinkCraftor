If you’ve started your hunt for the best stretch mark cream, you’ve likely noticed that the many options on the market can be overwhelming. Combine that with conflicting information about which skincare products are pregnancy-safe and what will actually work to get rid of stretch marks, and you may want to give up the search entirely. But don’t give up just yet. To help make the decision easier for moms-to-be, I did the research on everything you need to know when buying stretch mark cream, which options are the best, and what to look out for to ensure they’re pregnancy-safe.

According to theAmerican Academy of Dermatology (AAD), stretch marks appear when rapid changes in elastin and collagen production, such as a growing belly, cause the skin to rupture, scar, and then heal. Many factors impact whether you get stretch marks, including hormone levels and genetics, but creams and topical remedies can offer some relief.

"While there's no magic bullet cream to definitively prevent stretch marks, some products may help soothe itchy skin during pregnancy and may also improve the appearance of stretch marks," says Annabelle Garcia, M.D., a board-certified dermatologist atSonterra Dermatology.

How I Chose the Best Stretch Mark Creams

To ensure the safety and efficacy of the stretch mark creams on my list, I consulted several dermatologists, including Garcia and Emily Wood, M.D., a board-certified dermatologist at Westlake Dermatology. They provided guidance on which ingredients are safe to use during pregnancy, and I cross-referenced this with the AAD and Cleveland Clinic' recommendations. Through my research, I ensured that all the stretch mark creams featured were free of the following:

  • Retinol
  • Salicylic acid
  • Alpha hydroxy acids (AHAs)

I also prioritized products that contained at least one of these ingredients, which have been shown to help with skin elasticity:

  • Shea butter
  • Cocoa butter or coconut oil
  • Jojoba oil
  • Vitamin E
  • Centella asiatica extract
  • Hyaluronic acid
  • Aloe vera gel

I further narrowed down my list based on results from the2024 Pregnancy-Safe Beauty Awards, where over 700 moms voted on their favorite stretch mark cream. Finally, I spoke to two moms and looked to the What to Expect community to see what moms-to-be were using and what they found effective. Below are my recommendations for the best stretch mark creams on the market today.

A Note About Stretch Mark Creams

It’s important to remember that there isn’t a singular solution for stretch marks that works for everyone. TheAAD, along with the doctors I interviewed, states that whether or not you get stretch marks, and the severity of those stretch marks, depends on many factors beyond our control, such as hormones or genetics. As a result, a bit of trial and error may be necessary to find what truly works for you.

While creams containing centella asiatica are a good place to start due to their proven clinical efficacy for preventing stretch marks, remedies with popular ingredients like vitamin E or cocoa butter can help reduce the appearance of stretch marks if they do appear. The AAD recommends massaging the cream into the skin when you apply it, as massage can enhance the cream's effectiveness.

At the end of the day, though, none of these remedies are likely to cause negative effects, as long as you test a small patch on your skin to ensure you don’t have a reaction. Dr. Garcia notes that many of these remedies can at least alleviate itchy skin that often accompanies pregnancy. For others, these creams offer a healthy dose of hydration, which is important for maintaining your skin barrier, both during pregnancy and beyond

FAQs Best Stretch Mark Creams

What cream is best for pregnancy stretch marks?

According to the Cleveland Clinic, there’s some research suggesting home remedies with aloe vera gel, hyaluronic acid and centella asiatica herb may help moms and moms-to-be treat or prevent stretch marks. That being said, pregnant women should speak with their healthcare providers before trying out a stretch mark cream.

When should you start using stretch mark creams during pregnancy?

“I would recommend starting to use a stretch mark cream as soon as you know you are pregnant,” Dr. Wood says. This way, you can start treating skin as soon as hormones begin to fluctuate and skin begins to stretch.

What if stretch mark cream isn’t working?

If you’re not seeing results from one of these top-rated creams, ask your dermatologist about in-office treatments such as microneedling and laser to help reduce the appearance of stretch marks.
